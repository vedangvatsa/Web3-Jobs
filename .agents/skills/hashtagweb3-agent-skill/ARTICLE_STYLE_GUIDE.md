# Article Style Guide - Hashtag Web3 Playbook

## Core Principles

All articles should be **informative, credible, and direct**. No marketing fluff or AI slop.

## Banned Phrases (Replace These)

These phrases add no information and make content sound like marketing copy:

- **"revolutionize/revolutionary"** - Say what actually changed instead
- **"game-changer"** - Describe the actual benefit
- **"unlock"** - Use "enable," "allow," or "make possible"
- **"seamless"** - Say what specifically is easier
- **"cutting-edge"** - Describe the actual technical advancement
- **"unprecedented"** - State the specific new capability
- **"supercharge/amplify/accelerate"** - Use concrete metrics
- **"the future of X"** - Be specific about what's changing
- **"elevate your career"** - Describe the actual career improvement
- **"Communication is the lifeblood"** - Just say "communication matters"
- **"the best ideas come from unexpected combinations"** - Remove unless backed by data
- **"authentic," "authentic connection"** - Show don't tell
- **"the ultimate X"** - Remove superlatives without evidence
- **"transform your life/career"** - Be specific about outcomes
- **"limitless possibilities"** - Remove vague language

## What TO Include Instead

✅ **Specific outcomes**: "This tool saves 2 hours per week on transcription"
✅ **Concrete examples**: "You can now earn 0.5 cents per read vs $0.00 previously"  
✅ **Technical details**: How it works, not how amazing it is
✅ **Real constraints**: What doesn't work, limitations, trade-offs
✅ **Data/evidence**: When making claims
✅ **Who should use this**: Clear audience definition
✅ **Honest assessment**: Pros and cons

## Structure

1. **What is it**: Define clearly in 1-2 sentences
2. **Who it's for**: Specific audience
3. **How it works**: Mechanics, not marketing
4. **Pros & cons**: Trade-offs matter
5. **How to use/get started**: Practical next steps
6. **FAQ**: Direct answers

## Length

Don't cut words to remove fluff. Instead, **replace fluff with substance**:
- Remove: "Communication is the lifeblood of teams"
- Add: "In distributed teams, async communication reduces meeting time by ~30%"

## Examples

❌ **Before**: "This revolutionary tool will unlock your productivity and transform how you work remotely."

✅ **After**: "This tool transcribes meetings and generates summaries with action items, saving 45 minutes per week on note-taking and meeting recap emails."
