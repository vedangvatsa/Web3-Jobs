Aave is a decentralized finance (DeFi) protocol that allows users to lend and borrow cryptocurrencies without intermediaries.

## Company Overview

Aave (which means "ghost" in Finnish) was founded in 2017 by Stani Kulechov. Originally launched as ETHLend in 2017, the project rebranded to Aave in 2020. It has become one of the largest and most established DeFi protocols in the cryptocurrency ecosystem.

## Products and Services

**Core Protocol:**
- **Lending Markets**: Users can deposit crypto assets to earn interest
- **Borrowing**: Access to loans using crypto collateral
- **Flash Loans**: Uncollateralized loans that must be repaid within one transaction block
- **Rate Switching**: Ability to switch between stable and variable interest rates
- **Aave V3**: Latest version with improved capital efficiency and cross-chain functionality

**Additional Products:**
- **Aave GHO**: Native decentralized stablecoin
- **Aave Governance**: Decentralized governance through AAVE token holders
- **Aave Arc**: Permissioned pool for institutions
- **Aave Safety Module**: Insurance mechanism for the protocol

## Technology

Aave operates on multiple blockchain networks including:
- Ethereum
- Polygon
- Avalanche
- Arbitrum
- Optimism
- And several other Layer 1 and Layer 2 networks

The protocol uses smart contracts to automate lending and borrowing, eliminating the need for traditional financial intermediaries.

## Governance

Aave is governed by AAVE token holders who can propose and vote on changes to the protocol. This includes decisions about:
- Protocol parameters
- Asset listings
- Treasury management
- Protocol upgrades
- Risk parameters

## Market Position

Aave is consistently ranked among the top DeFi protocols by total value locked (TVL), often competing with protocols like MakerDAO and Compound for the largest market share in decentralized lending.
