Peer-to-peer lending protocol optimizer built on top of Aave and Compound.

## Company Overview

Morpho Labs develops the Morpho protocol, which optimizes lending rates by matching lenders and borrowers peer-to-peer on top of existing protocols like Aave and Compound. Morpho Blue provides permissionless lending markets.
