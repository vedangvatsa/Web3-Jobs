Movement Labs is the creator of the Movement Network, an ecosystem of Modular Move-based blockchains designed to bring the safety and performance of the Move programming language to Ethereum and beyond. Its flagship Move L2 combines MoveVM parallel execution, formal verification, and sub-second finality with Ethereum shared security and native EVM compatibility through Fractal. Backed by top venture funds, Movement Labs empowers developers to build secure, high-throughput consumer and DeFi applications.
