O2 is a cryptocurrency exchange focused on building superior trading infrastructure. The platform aims to offer a better exchange experience compared to traditional platforms that often rely on extensive teams rather than optimized technology.

## Products and Focus
O2 emphasizes building a better, faster, and more efficient exchange. The platform positions itself as a new solution in the crypto trading space, highlighting its autonomous and technologically advanced approach to matching engines and trade execution.
