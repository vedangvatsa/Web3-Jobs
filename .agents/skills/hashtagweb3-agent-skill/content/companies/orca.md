Orca is a decentralized exchange built on the Solana blockchain. It offers users a platform for trading cryptocurrencies with low fees and fast transaction speeds. The protocol uses an automated market maker (AMM) model, allowing users to provide liquidity and earn rewards.

Orca features a user-friendly interface, making it accessible for both new and experienced traders. The platform supports various trading pairs and has integrated tools for liquidity providers to optimize their returns. Orca aims to enhance the trading experience in the DeFi space through its new features and community-driven approach.

The company currently has job listings for positions such as Paralegal and Product Manager, indicating a focus on expanding its operational and product development teams.
