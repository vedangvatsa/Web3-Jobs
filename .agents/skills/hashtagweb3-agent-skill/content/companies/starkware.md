StarkWare is known for its development of Layer 2 scaling solutions for Ethereum, using zero-knowledge rollups. The company aims to enhance transaction throughput and reduce costs on the Ethereum network. Its flagship products include StarkEx, a scalability engine for decentralized applications, and StarkNet, a permissionless decentralized ZK-Rollup that enables developers to build scalable dApps.

The company focuses on providing tools that allow developers to create efficient and secure blockchain applications. StarkWare's technology is designed to improve user experience by enabling faster transactions and lower fees. The company has gained attention for its new approach to blockchain scalability and security, making it a notable player in the Web3 ecosystem.
