Stellar is designed to enable cross-border transactions and improve access to financial services. The platform allows users to send money across borders quickly and at low cost. Stellar's technology connects banks, payment systems, and people, enabling efficient transfers of value.

Stellar's network uses a unique consensus mechanism called the Stellar Consensus Protocol, which allows for fast transaction confirmations. The platform supports various digital assets and enables the issuance of tokens, making it versatile for different financial applications.
