Stripe offers a suite of payment processing services that enable businesses to accept payments online. Their platform supports various payment methods, including credit cards, debit cards, and digital wallets. Stripe's products include tools for managing subscriptions, invoicing, and fraud prevention.

The company is known for its developer-friendly API, which allows businesses to integrate payment processing smooth into their websites and applications. Stripe also provides solutions for marketplaces, enabling businesses to manage payments between buyers and sellers.

Stripe has job openings in various roles, including Professional Services Engagement Manager, Software Engineer, and Risk Strategist. The company emphasizes a collaborative work environment and focuses on building new financial infrastructure for the internet.
