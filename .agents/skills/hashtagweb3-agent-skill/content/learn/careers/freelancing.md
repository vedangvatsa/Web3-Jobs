## Why freelancing works in Web3

Web3 is built for freelancing. Projects are global, payments are instant (stablecoins), and reputation is on-chain. You do not need a recruiter or a résumé - you need a wallet and a track record.

Three paths to Web3 freelancing:

## 1. Bounties

Bounties are one-off tasks with fixed payments. They range from $50 (write a blog post) to $50,000+ (build a major feature).

| Platform | What it offers | Payment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gitcoin | Open-source development bounties | Crypto |
| Dework | DAO task management and bounties | Crypto |
| Layer3 | Learn-to-earn quests and bounties | Crypto + NFTs |
| Immunefi | Bug bounty programs for DeFi security | $1K-$10M+ |

**Start here**: Pick a protocol you use, check their GitHub issues labeled "good first issue" or "bounty", and start small.

## 2. DAO contributions

Many DAOs have contributor programs. You join a working group, contribute consistently, and get paid from the treasury.

Common DAO contributor roles:
- **Writing**: Newsletter, governance summaries, documentation
- **Development**: Build features, fix bugs, create tools
- **Design**: UI/UX design, brand assets, presentation templates
- **Analytics**: Dune dashboards, treasury reports, market research

DAOs like MakerDAO, Gitcoin, and Bankless have structured contributor programs with monthly payments.

## 3. Grants

Grants are lump-sum funding for bigger projects. If you have an idea that benefits the ecosystem, you can apply.

| Grant program | Focus | Typical size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ethereum Foundation | Research, tooling, education | $5K-500K |
| Uniswap Grants | DeFi tooling, analytics | $10K-100K |
| Optimism RPGF | Public goods funding | $5K-500K |
| Aave Grants | Lending ecosystem tools | $5K-50K |

## Getting paid

Web3 freelancing payments are typically:
- **Stablecoins** (USDC, USDT) sent to your wallet - instant, global, no bank needed
- **Native tokens** (UNI, OP, ARB) - may appreciate or depreciate

Set up invoicing: tools like Request Network and Utopia Labs are designed for Web3 payments.

## Key takeaways

- Web3 freelancing does not require a full-time job - start with bounties.
- Bounty platforms (Gitcoin, Dework, Immunefi) list paid tasks of all sizes.
- DAO contributor programs offer regular paid work.
- Grants fund larger projects ($5K-500K) that benefit the ecosystem.
- Payments are in stablecoins - instant, global, no bank required.
