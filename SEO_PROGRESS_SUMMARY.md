## Strategy Notes

### Contextual Linking Success Factors
1. **Natural Integration:** Links flow with content, not forced at end
2. **Relevant Anchors:** Link text is descriptive ("DeFi Development Careers" not "click here")
3. **Strategic Distribution:** 1 link every 100-200 words (not too sparse, not link spam)
4. **Contextual Relevance:** Only link to actually related content
5. **User-First:** Helps user journey (resume → interview prep → salary guide → job board)

### Meta Description Formula That Works
- **Location Guides:** `[Location] Jobs | [Salary Range] | [Hub Type] 2026. [Specific Roles]. [Unique Benefit].`
- **Role Guides:** `[Role] Jobs | [Salary Range] | [Industry Focus]. [Key Skills]. [Market Signal].`
- **Skill Guides:** `[Skill] Guide | [Use Cases] | [2026 Relevance]. [Learning Path]. [Career Path].`
- **Career Guides:** `[Topic] | [Benefit Outcome] | [Action/Credential]. [Specific Deliverable]. [Call to Action].`
