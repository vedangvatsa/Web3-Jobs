Crypto platforms started selling exposure to Anthropic stock the day before the AI company's Nasdaq debut, with Ondo announcing a token that goes live when trading opens and Binance Wallet's $500 million subscription for tokenized Anthropic closing early after hitting its cap.

Ondo [said Wednesday](https://chainwire.org/2026/11/18/ondo-stocks-to-launch-anton-with-24-7-mint-and-redeem-live-on-ipo-day/) that ANTon will launch on Ondo Stocks on the day Anthropic begins trading on Nasdaq, giving eligible investors outside the United States exposure to the listing from the first day. The token will support minting and redemption around the clock, weekends included, starting at $1 with zero mint fees and reduced redemption fees, though blockchain network fees still apply.

"The world has watched Anthropic grow for years with no way to participate. On IPO day, Wall Street gets its allocation and everyone else waits," Ian De Bode, acting CEO and president of Ondo Finance, said in the announcement. He said ANTon "mints and redeems after New York goes home."

Ondo Global Markets is not affiliated with Anthropic and is not participating in the offering, and the release states that ANTon is not issued, sponsored or endorsed by Anthropic. ANTon will join more than 450 stocks and exchange-traded funds on Ondo Stocks, which the company says passed $1 billion in total value locked and operates across Ethereum, Solana and BNB Chain.

Binance Wallet opened an eight-hour sale Wednesday for up to $500 million in tokenized Anthropic shares, according to [Bitcoin.com News](https://news.bitcoin.com/finance/ondo-launch-tokenized-anthropic-stock-ipo-demand-nears-500m/), with a minimum investment of 100 USDC and a 5% fee. The tokens, ANTx, are issued by Backed or its affiliates and give price exposure without direct share ownership, voting rights or dividend entitlements. Kraken has also opened requests for ANTx in eligible markets.

The subscription filled its 500 million USDC cap and closed early on Wednesday, hours after opening, Bankless [reported](https://www.bankless.com/read/news/crypto-lines-up-for-tokenized-anthropic-before-its-ipo). The same report said Bitget runs its own rANT version of the product and that its public round drew more than $100 million.

Buyers in these allocation sales pledge funds at the IPO price plus the fee and receive tokens only if the issuer lands real shares from underwriters, with no guarantee of how many shares appear. Bankless noted that Binance's own FAQ says the exchange does not verify the backing assets and that xStocks may distribute bStocks instead. The caution follows June, when Binance, Bybit and Bitget Wallet refunded tokenized SpaceX IPO subscriptions after xStocks could not secure enough shares; none of those venues has said how many Anthropic shares are lined up this time.

Anthropic [priced the offering](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering) at $83.00 per share on Wednesday, above the $70 to $78 range it had set earlier in the week. The company said the offering consists of 980 million Class A shares, with 956,351,354 sold by Anthropic and about 23.6 million sold by certain stockholders, and that trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market begins Nov 19 under the symbol ANT. Anthropic also granted underwriters a 30-day option to buy up to an additional 147 million shares.

CNBC [reported](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-ipo-ai-offering-price.html) that the raise reaches $81.3 billion, which it called the largest offering on record, ahead of SpaceX's June IPO, which took in $75 billion initially and $85.7 billion after underwriters exercised their overallotment. CNBC put Anthropic's fully diluted market capitalization at about $1.5 trillion.

Demand reports had set expectations before the pricing. Bitcoin.com News [wrote](https://news.bitcoin.com/finance/ondo-launch-tokenized-anthropic-stock-ipo-demand-nears-500m/), citing a CNBC report, that institutional investors submitted roughly $400 billion in orders and retail demand approached $100 billion, both nonbinding indications of interest rather than confirmed purchases. The company's amended filing shows $33.24 billion in revenue for the nine months ended Sept 30, up about 1,200% from a year earlier, and a $104 billion net loss driven mostly by fair-value changes in convertible notes and derivative liabilities.

Pre-IPO perpetual futures were pricing above the original range. On Wednesday morning, contracts on trade.xyz's Hyperliquid market and on Coinbase traded between about $96 and $99, roughly 24% to 27% above the top of the announced range, according to Bankless. Those contracts are cash-settled bets with no shares behind them.

Ondo says ANTon goes live on Ondo Stocks once ANT begins trading Thursday. Both ANTon and ANTx exclude US investors under their current frameworks, and Ondo's release states that its tokens give holders economic exposure rather than the underlying shares.
