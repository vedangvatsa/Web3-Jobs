Bitmine bought 7,653 ETH over the week ending Nov. 15, 2026 and now holds 6,077,745 ether, the company said in a press release on Nov. 16. At the reference price of $1,823 per coin that the release used, the stack is worth about $11.1 billion, and the company's crypto, cash, marketable securities and small equity positions together total $11.8 billion.

The total is down from $14.3 billion a week earlier, and [Decrypt reported](https://decrypt.co/383364/tom-lee-blames-nvidia-anthropic-ipo-ethereum-plunge-bitmine-buys) that almost all of the drop comes from the fall in ether's price rather than from any selling. The reference price in the release was $1,823 per ETH, quoted from Coinbase. Ether traded 28% lower over two weeks, a move chairman Tom Lee called a "waterfall decline in price" that he said does not match the flow of fundamental news, including tokenization projects announced by banks and financial institutions. The [release](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/bitmine-immersion-technologies-bmnr-announces-eth-holdings-reach-6-08-million-tokens-and-total-crypto-and-total-cash--marketable-securities-holdings-of-11-8-billion-302940161.html) carries the quotes in full.

Lee tied the selling to two calendar events. He wrote that recent developments at Nvidia are triggering margin selling, particularly in Asia, and that investors are raising cash ahead of Anthropic's initial public offering, which is planned for pricing on Nov. 18, 2026. He compared the pattern to the run-up to SpaceX's $75 billion IPO on June 11, 2026: ether fell 39% in the eight weeks before that pricing, fell 29% in the final two weeks and bottomed three trading days ahead of it. Ahead of the Anthropic listing, Lee wrote, ether is down 39% over eight weeks and 28% over two weeks.

Bitmine's position still equals 4.97% of the 122.2 million ether in supply, which the company describes as 99% of the goal it calls the "Alchemy of 5%". The treasury strategy began on June 30, 2025, and Lee said in the release that the company has bought ether every week since then. Bitmine was added to the Russell 1000 index on June 26, 2026, according to the same document.

Staking now covers most of the bag. Bitmine reported 5,227,037 staked ETH as of Nov. 15, worth $9.5 billion at the reference price, or about 86% of the total holdings, run through its MAVAN platform. Lee put annualized staking revenue at roughly $245 million on that basis and said the figure would reach about $285 million once the entire position is staked, using a 2.57% seven-day yield. Those projections sit in the forward-looking section of the release, which warns that results may differ.

Alongside the ether, Bitmine listed 216 bitcoin, $448 million in cash and marketable securities, a $180 million stake in Beast Industries and a $67 million stake in Eightco Holdings, which trades on Nasdaq under the ticker ORBS. The release also places Bitmine second among global crypto treasuries behind Strategy, which it says holds 849,327 BTC, and notes that the company's Series A preferred stock trades on the NYSE under BMNP.

Decrypt's Nov. 16 write-up of the update reached the same headline numbers: 6,077,745 ETH, 4.97% of supply, and a purchase of 7,653 ETH worth roughly $14 million during the week. The outlet also noted that 86% of the holdings sit staked on MAVAN, with projected annualized revenue near $245 million at current levels.

The update is the latest in a weekly series of holdings releases that began with the treasury strategy in mid-2025, and it arrived two days before Anthropic is scheduled to price its offering. Bitmine's own figures put the holdings at 6,077,745 ETH and total crypto, cash and marketable securities at $11.8 billion as of 3:00 p.m. ET on Nov. 15.
