Tyler Robert Buchanan, a 24-year-old hacker tied to the Scattered Spider collective, was sentenced Tuesday to six years in U.S. federal prison for a phishing and SIM-swapping scheme that stole at least $11.6 million in crypto. U.S. District Judge John W. Holcomb handed down the 72-month sentence in Santa Ana, California, and set a restitution hearing for Dec. 11, when the judge will determine the amount, [Decrypt reported](https://decrypt.co/383522/scattered-spider-hacker-sentenced-to-6-years-over-11-6m-in-stolen-crypto), citing the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Central District of California. The office announced the outcome in a [post](https://decrypt.co/383522/scattered-spider-hacker-sentenced-to-6-years-over-11-6m-in-stolen-crypto) describing a "British national sentenced to six years in federal prison for hacking into companies and stealing at least $11 million in virtual currency." Buchanan is the fifth and final defendant to be sentenced in the case, [Bitcoin Magazine wrote](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/scattered-spider-hacker-six-years-crypto-theft).

Buchanan, of Dundee in Scotland, pleaded guilty in April to conspiracy to commit wire fraud and aggravated identity theft. Prosecutors first put the group's take at about $8 million; the $11.6 million figure is the loss authorities calculated for the full scheme. The U.S. Attorney's Office release does not [break the haul down by coin](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/scattered-spider-hacker-six-years-crypto-theft), and prosecutors did not name which cryptocurrencies were taken.

The thefts ran from September 2021 to April 2023, when Buchanan and his co-conspirators sent hundreds of phishing texts to employees of at least a dozen companies, including telecoms, technology firms and crypto businesses. The messages posed as the employer or an IT contractor and linked to fake login pages that captured usernames, passwords and other personal information. The group used the stolen data to [identify individual crypto holders](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/scattered-spider-hacker-six-years-crypto-theft) before striking them. The BBC reported that Buchanan set up a Telegram bot to collect the credentials as workers handed them over, according to [Bitcoin Magazine](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/scattered-spider-hacker-six-years-crypto-theft). The crew then ran SIM swaps, moving victims' phone numbers to SIM cards they controlled so they could intercept two-factor authentication codes and drain wallets. Prosecutors said the captured login details were forwarded to a Telegram channel that Buchanan and another co-conspirator controlled, [prosecutors said](https://decrypt.co/383522/scattered-spider-hacker-sentenced-to-6-years-over-11-6m-in-stolen-crypto).

The largest single theft took $1.67 million from one victim in December 2022, the BBC said in the account [Decrypt cited](https://decrypt.co/383522/scattered-spider-hacker-sentenced-to-6-years-over-11-6m-in-stolen-crypto). The money came from [individual victims rather than the companies whose employees were phished](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/scattered-spider-hacker-six-years-crypto-theft). Police Scotland searched Buchanan's bedroom in April 2023 after a request from the FBI, and investigators found names and addresses of victims along with a file of crypto seed phrases and login details for one victim's account, prosecutors said. "Those thefts had severe, life-altering consequences for many victims," prosecutors argued in a sentencing memo.

The Justice Department charged five defendants together in November 2024, and a department spokesperson said at the time that they were suspected members of Scattered Spider, the group behind attacks including the 2023 breach of MGM Resorts, Decrypt wrote. Co-defendant Noah Urban, of Palm Coast, Florida, is serving 10 years, while Ahmed Hossam Eldin Elbadawy and Evans Onyeaka Osiebo each received 45 months and Joel Martin Evans got two years. All four co-defendants [have pleaded guilty and are serving time](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/scattered-spider-hacker-six-years-crypto-theft).

Spanish authorities arrested Buchanan at Palma de Mallorca Airport in 2024 as he tried to board a private flight to Naples; he was extradited from Spain in April 2025 and has been in U.S. custody since. Bloomberg News reported the extradition and Reuters relayed it, [Bitcoin Magazine wrote](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/scattered-spider-hacker-six-years-crypto-theft). He [left Britain in May 2023](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/scattered-spider-hacker-six-years-crypto-theft), a month after the Scottish search, and began hacking as a teenager.

Scattered Spider is a loose-knit community of young, English-speaking hackers [blamed for a string of high-profile cyberattacks](https://decrypt.co/383522/scattered-spider-hacker-sentenced-to-6-years-over-11-6m-in-stolen-crypto) and [suspected of breaking into dozens of U.S. companies](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/scattered-spider-hacker-six-years-crypto-theft). The Justice Department's release on the sentencing [does not use the group's name](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/scattered-spider-hacker-six-years-crypto-theft). The case grew out of a [years-long investigation involving authorities in the United Kingdom, the United States, Spain and Canada](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/scattered-spider-hacker-six-years-crypto-theft). Two other members were jailed in Britain in July for a series of attacks, including one on Transport for London that caused 39 million pounds in damage, [according to the report](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/scattered-spider-hacker-six-years-crypto-theft). U.S. authorities keep pursuing alleged members of the collective: in July, a 19-year-old suspect was [extradited from Finland](https://decrypt.co/383522/scattered-spider-hacker-sentenced-to-6-years-over-11-6m-in-stolen-crypto) over an $8 million crypto ransom demand against a luxury jeweler.
