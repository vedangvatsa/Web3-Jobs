The Czech National Bank (CNB) has successfully concluded pilot trials of tokenized digital bonds, evaluating the mechanics of distributed ledger technology for debt issuance, secondary trading, and delivery-versus-payment settlement.

The central bank conducted the testing to determine how tokenized bonds perform against conventional sovereign and municipal debt systems. The trials demonstrated that digital issuance on distributed ledgers can compress multi-day settlement cycles into near-instant clearing, eliminate counterparty risk, and automate coupon payments via smart contracts.

The initiative comes as European central banks and monetary authorities increasingly test wholesale settlement frameworks, examining both wholesale central bank digital currency (wCBDC) and distributed ledger rails to modernize European capital markets.
