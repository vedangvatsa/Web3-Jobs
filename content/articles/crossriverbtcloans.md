Cross River Bank, a U.S. state-chartered bank known for powering fintech infrastructure, has entered the digital asset credit market with a Bitcoin-backed lending product offering loans of up to $250,000.

The program allows qualifying borrowers to pledge Bitcoin as collateral to access dollar liquidity without selling their underlying holdings or triggering immediate capital gains events. Collateral is held with regulated custody partners, with automated loan-to-value monitoring and margin-call thresholds built into the underwriting framework to manage market volatility.

Historically, crypto-collateralized lending was dominated by non-bank crypto lenders, several of which collapsed during prior market downturns due to opaque rehypothecation practices and weak risk controls. By contrast, Cross River Bank brings federal and state banking oversight, transparent collateral segregation, and institutional underwriting standards to the product.

For long-term Bitcoin holders, founders, and high-net-worth individuals, the offering provides a regulated alternative for accessing working capital or personal liquidity. Borrowers retain exposure to Bitcoin price movements while paying structured interest rates backed by overcollateralized reserves.

The launch marks a notable step by a regulated U.S. bank into crypto-collateralized lending and underscores how traditional financial institutions are increasingly integrating digital assets into core credit products.
