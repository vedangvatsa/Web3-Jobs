Cross River Bank, a U.S. state-chartered bank known for powering fintech infrastructure, has entered the digital asset credit market with a Bitcoin-backed lending product offering loans of up to $250,000.

The program allows qualifying borrowers to pledge Bitcoin as collateral to access dollar liquidity without selling their underlying holdings. Collateral is held with regulated custody partners, with automated loan-to-value monitoring and margin-call thresholds built into the underwriting framework.

The launch marks a notable step by a regulated U.S. bank into crypto-collateralized consumer and business lending, a segment previously dominated by non-bank crypto lenders.
