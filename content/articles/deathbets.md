The US Senate passed the DEATH BETS Act on November 19, 2026, by voice vote, sending the House a bill that would bar exchanges and clearinghouses registered with the Commodity Futures Trading Commission from listing contracts tied to war, terrorism, assassination, US military operations, or events expected to be settled by a person's death. The official engrossed text of [S. 4035](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119s4035es/html/BILLS-119s4035es.htm) carries the line "Passed the Senate November 19, 2026."

Senator Adam Schiff, a California Democrat, introduced the Discouraging Exploitative Assassination, Tragedy, and Harm Betting in Event Trading Systems Act in March, and Senator John Curtis of Utah, a Republican, cosponsored it. "With this bill, Congress is making it clear that these bets should be illegal," Schiff wrote on X after the vote, [Decrypt reported](https://decrypt.co/383700/senate-passes-bill-banning-prediction-market-bets-on-war-terrorism-and-death).

The bill amends section 5c of the Commodity Exchange Act. The [engrossed text](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119s4035es/html/BILLS-119s4035es.htm) states that a registered entity may not list for trading or accept for clearing a contract based on an excluded commodity that expressly references terrorism, assassination, war, United States Armed Forces military operations, or any similar activity the Commission determines by rule to be necessary and appropriate. It also bars contracts that, at the time of listing, are reasonably expected to resolve principally based on a death of an individual. A separate section says nothing in the act affects the permissibility of other event contracts covered by existing law, including those involving gaming.

The Senate adopted an amendment from Schiff by unanimous consent just before passage, [Cointelegraph reported](https://cointelegraph.com/news/us-senate-advances-prediction-market-restrictions-with-death-bets-act). Curtis said the substitute amendment adds a rule of construction so the bill cannot be read as changing the legal status of other event contracts. "There should be no financial incentive to profit from terrorism, war, or the death of another human being," Curtis [said in a press release](https://www.curtis.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/curtis-applauds-senate-passage-of-bipartisan-death-bets-act/).

Prediction markets such as Kalshi and Polymarket let users trade on the outcome of real-world events, from elections and sports to geopolitics. Markets on wars and the fates of world leaders have drawn criticism from lawmakers, who have argued that they let people profit from tragedy and could reward insiders with knowledge of military plans, [Decrypt reported](https://decrypt.co/383700/senate-passes-bill-banning-prediction-market-bets-on-war-terrorism-and-death).

Schiff first introduced the bill in March, when six Polymarket traders reportedly earned $1 million betting on the timing of US strikes against Iran, with several wagers placed just hours before the first reported explosions in Tehran, according to [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/us-senate-advances-prediction-market-restrictions-with-death-bets-act). Decrypt separately [noted](https://decrypt.co/383700/senate-passes-bill-banning-prediction-market-bets-on-war-terrorism-and-death) that Polymarket users have been accused of insider trading after making $2.4 million on Iran-related predictions. Schiff [warned in March](https://cointelegraph.com/news/us-senate-advances-prediction-market-restrictions-with-death-bets-act) that allowing traders to profit from violent events could encourage the misuse of classified information, threaten national security and create incentives for violence.

Curtis, in announcing the substitute amendment, called prediction markets an effort to blur the line between legitimate financial markets and outright gambling. "Calling something a financial contract does not change the fact that it is gambling," he said in the [press release](https://www.curtis.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/curtis-applauds-senate-passage-of-bipartisan-death-bets-act/). Curtis argued that prediction platforms must not be allowed to exploit federal financial regulations to circumvent state and tribal gambling laws. He and Schiff have also introduced a separate measure, the Prediction Markets Are Gambling Act, which would prohibit sports betting and casino-style gambling contracts on federally regulated exchanges, the release said.

The marketplaces face other legal pressure too. Kalshi was sued after refusing to pay out on a [market tied to the death of Iran's then-Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei](https://decrypt.co/383700/senate-passes-bill-banning-prediction-market-bets-on-war-terrorism-and-death). In June, the [CFTC proposed rules](https://decrypt.co/383700/senate-passes-bill-banning-prediction-market-bets-on-war-terrorism-and-death) that would ban markets on outcomes that could come about through war or assassination, including vaguely worded bets on when US adversaries would be "out of office" that Kalshi and Polymarket had used to sidestep existing bans on war-related contracts.

The CFTC has pressed its authority in court as well. The agency [first sued three states in April](https://decrypt.co/383700/senate-passes-bill-banning-prediction-market-bets-on-war-terrorism-and-death) to defend its oversight of event contracts and has now taken 12 states to court, most recently Louisiana. [New York has sued Coinbase and Gemini](https://decrypt.co/383700/senate-passes-bill-banning-prediction-market-bets-on-war-terrorism-and-death) over their prediction markets. The bill now moves to the House, where it must pass before it can be sent to President Donald Trump for his signature, Cointelegraph said.
