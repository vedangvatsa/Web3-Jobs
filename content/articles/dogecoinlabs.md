Dogecoin Labs launched Tuesday in partnership with the Dogecoin Foundation, consolidating the meme coin's largest consumer products, intellectual property, and development teams into a single independent company led by MyDoge founder Jordan Jefferson as CEO.

The new company will own and operate three products: the MyDoge wallet, the DogeOS app platform, and Such App, a merchant-payments tool. Assets and talent were contributed by both the Foundation and House of Doge, the Foundation's commercial arm, [Decrypt reported](https://decrypt.co/381541/dogecoin-gets-dedicated-company-build-ecosystem).

"No other digital asset has Dogecoin's combination of liquidity, community and cultural reach, and its potential has barely been tapped," [Jefferson said](https://decrypt.co/381541/dogecoin-gets-dedicated-company-build-ecosystem), adding that the goal is "to build the economy that ambition calls for."

## What the three products do

The plan connects three parts of a DOGE business ecosystem. Developers build applications on DogeOS, holders find them through MyDoge, and merchants use Such App to accept payments. Such App's beta already supports invoices and in-person QR payments, [CoinDesk reported](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/10/21/dogecoin-wallet-app-teams-unite-in-push-to-give-doge-more-everyday-uses).

MyDoge said it has grown to more than 500,000 users without paid acquisition. Its V3 update connects the wallet to applications being built on DogeOS. The announcement does not specify how many wallet users are active or have tried those applications.

DogeOS opened its public test network on Sept. 30, allowing developers to build Ethereum-style lending services, exchanges, and games using test DOGE. Dogecoin itself handles coin transfers, so those applications run on a separate system. Three weeks after the testnet launch, DogeOS's public user guide still marks its featured applications as "coming soon."

DogeOS's planned security link to the Dogecoin blockchain also needs further work. DogeOS wants Dogecoin's own network to check mathematical proofs that its applications ran correctly. A proposed rule change enabling that remains a draft. Until the link is implemented, users will have to rely on DogeOS's selected operators and hardware to check that applications work.

## Who is behind the new company

Jefferson founded MyDoge, which has positioned itself as one of the larger non-custodial Dogecoin wallets. House of Doge, the commercial entity the Foundation set up to pursue business opportunities around the token, contributed product IP and team members. The Foundation keeps its open-source development work, advocacy, and community programs, while Labs takes on product delivery and commercial partnerships, Decrypt noted.

The structure gives Labs a clearer corporate face for deals with exchanges, payment processors, and app developers, areas where the Foundation's nonprofit status and decentralized community governance have limited how quickly it can act. House of Doge had already been exploring sponsorship and brand partnerships before the reorganization.

Neither the announcement nor the Foundation's post specifies how the equity or revenue from Labs products will flow back to the nonprofit side, or whether token holders have any claim on Labs proceeds. Foundations linked to other major tokens have used similar structures to fund open-source work through commercial subsidiaries.

## Foundation role and market context

The Foundation will continue its open-source development support, advocacy, and community work while Labs concentrates on products and commercial opportunities. The reorganization arrives during a soft stretch for the token. DOGE traded around $0.084 on Tuesday, up 3.9% on the day, but down about 14% over the past month and off roughly 88% from its May 2021 record, Decrypt noted.

Dogecoin's institutional story has been mixed. A Dogecoin Foundation-backed 21Shares DOGE ETF launched on Nasdaq this year, though Bitwise later shut its own Dogecoin fund citing low demand. The token still ranks among the largest by market capitalization, sustained largely by brand recognition and an active retail community rather than on-chain application usage.

Prior to the Labs formation, the Foundation and House of Doge had operated as separate arms: one focused on open-source protocol maintenance and grants, the other on commercial deals and brand licensing. Consolidating product ownership under Labs ends the split for wallet and app work, leaving the Foundation focused on protocol-level contributions such as core client maintenance and the proposed rule change DogeOS needs for its security model.
