A wallet linked to the Ethereum Foundation sold 1,000 ETH on Nov. 10 and another 1,000 ETH the following day, according to on-chain analytics firm Lookonchain, which has tracked the address through a series of sales since October. Both batches were swapped through CoW Protocol for the USDS stablecoin rather than routed to a centralized exchange.

Lookonchain [posted](https://x.com/lookonchain/status/2119974163263947154) on Nov. 10 that the address had sold 1,000 ETH worth about $2.13 million after a two-week pause, noting that ETH had dropped 7.7% since the wallet's previous sale. The analytics firm identified the wallet as 0x5CBC and linked to a block explorer entry for the address. The second batch cleared the next day at a lower average. [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/is-ethereum-price-at-risk-as-foundation-linked-wallet-sells-more-eth/) reported that the Nov. 11 transaction brought in roughly 2.025 million USDS at an average of about $2,025 per ETH, while the prior day's sale averaged closer to $2,130. The stablecoins were returned to the same address through CoW Protocol's settlement contract, with later transfers involving a Gnosis Safe proxy wallet, that report said. Together the two days produced about $4.16 million, the outlet said.

[Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/ethereum-foundation-sells-2000-eth/) put the combined figure slightly lower, reporting that a post on X valued the two batches at $3.87 million while a research tally placed proceeds at roughly $4.14 million to $4.16 million. The difference comes down to which execution prices are counted across the day's swaps. Either way, the sales ended a quiet spell: the wallet had been inactive for about two weeks before the November activity.

The address is the same one Lookonchain [flagged on Oct. 26](https://x.com/lookonchain/status/2114559686221746457) after it sold 2,785 ETH for about $6.42 million at an average of $2,304. That activity involved several swaps through CoW Protocol, including individual sales of 500 ETH, 600 ETH and 1,000 ETH, according to crypto.news. Across the October sale and the November batches, the wallet has sold 4,785 ETH for roughly $10.58 million at the respective execution prices, the same report said. Even after the selling, the monitored address still held more than 24,000 ETH, worth about $51.46 million as of the sale dates, Crypto Briefing reported.

The Foundation has not commented on the individual transactions. Its published treasury policy targets a fiat operating buffer covering about 2.5 years of operational expenses, and it periodically converts portions of its ETH holdings into stablecoins to maintain that buffer, Crypto Briefing reported. The organization has also said it wants to rely less on open-market sales over time, with plans to shift toward yield-generating strategies including staking and DeFi to fund operations.

Sales by Foundation-linked wallets are not new. On Oct. 12, two such wallets transferred 4,393 ETH worth about $11.13 million to Kraken, an amount the analyst EmberCN traced to an Ethereum Foundation wallet that had distributed 59,751 ETH roughly ten years earlier, [crypto.news reported](https://crypto.news/is-ethereum-price-at-risk-as-foundation-linked-wallet-sells-more-eth/). One of the tracked wallets had moved 49,751 ETH to Kraken over the following decade at an average historical price of about $658, the analyst said. Unlike the CoW Protocol swaps, those transactions were exchange deposits with no confirmed sale at the time. The Foundation has historically used ETH from its treasury to fund ecosystem development, research, grants and operating costs.

The November sales came as ETH traded near $2,000. On Nov. 10, ether changed hands around $2,120 after U.S. spot Ethereum ETFs recorded $184.51 million in net outflows the previous day, according to SoSoValue figures [cited by crypto.news](https://crypto.news/is-ethereum-price-at-risk-as-foundation-linked-wallet-sells-more-eth/). Across the October sale and the two November days, the monitored address has parted with 4,785 ETH while still holding over 24,000.
