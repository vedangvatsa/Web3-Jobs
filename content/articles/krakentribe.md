Payward, the parent company of the Kraken exchange, said on 18 November it signed a definitive agreement to acquire Tribe Capital Management, a Menlo Park venture capital and asset-management firm with $2.7 billion in assets under management. [The announcement](https://www.payward.com/press-release/payward-to-acquire-tribe-capital) sets no purchase price and expects the deal to close in the first quarter of 2027, subject to customary closing conditions including required consents.

Tribe will sit inside Payward's Asset Management pillar, alongside Trading, Banking, Services and AI. Payward said it plans to build new private-market offerings on Tribe's platform, connecting investment sourcing and fund management with its own custody, trading, collateral and tokenization capabilities. The company framed the move as a response to customer demand for access to early-stage companies and private-market opportunities.

"Joining Payward gives us the opportunity to bring Tribe's investment approach to a global financial platform," Tribe co-founder Jonathan Hsu said in the release. He added that Tribe sees "a strong fit between our investment approach and Payward's infrastructure, distribution, and product-building capabilities."

Tribe was founded in 2018 by Arjun Sethi, Hsu and Ted Maidenberg, and invests across early- and late-stage private companies through commingled funds and co-investment vehicles, per the [same release](https://www.payward.com/press-release/payward-to-acquire-tribe-capital). Sethi is also Kraken's co-CEO and chairs Tribe, [Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/krakens-owner-payward-strikes-deal-for-tribe-capital-a-2-7-billion-venture-investor/) reported, noting that the announcement names him only as a founder and does not describe what part he played in the deal. The same account said the firm uses data science and artificial intelligence to pick its investments, and that it runs pooled funds alongside co-investment vehicles that let outside backers join individual deals.

The deal centers on capabilities. Tribe brings "investment sourcing, underwriting, portfolio construction, and fund-management capabilities" that complement Payward's existing infrastructure, the release said, and those skills would widen the investment products available across Payward's consumer, professional and institutional client base. [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-18-kraken-parent-payward-tribe-capital-443522) summarized the same addition as private-market sourcing, underwriting and fund management for the platform.

[The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-18-kraken-parent-payward-tribe-capital-443522) reported the same terms on 18 November, including the $2.7 billion figure and the first-quarter 2027 target, and said Payward did not disclose the financial terms. The firm invests in companies applying AI and advanced software across technology and financial infrastructure, with portfolio names including Shiprocket, SpaceX, Applied Intuition, Apollo.io and Kapital, according to Payward's release.

The advisors are named in the announcement. Moelis & Company served as financial advisor to Payward, with Jones Day and Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz as legal counsel and Morrison Foerster as regulatory counsel; Ropes & Gray advised Tribe on the legal side. Unchained's account of the deal named Moelis and Ropes & Gray as the two leads and did not list the additional firms.

For Payward, the deal extends a platform that already spans products including Kraken, NinjaTrader, Breakout, xStocks and CF Benchmarks. Payward describes itself as an integrated financial services and market infrastructure company and says the infrastructure layer behind Kraken also runs those separate product lines. Unchained [connected](https://unchainedcrypto.com/krakens-owner-payward-strikes-deal-for-tribe-capital-a-2-7-billion-venture-investor/) the acquisition to that tokenization push: its xStocks business now lists more than 1,500 tokenized assets after Payward added over 200 UK and European stocks earlier in November, and the outlet said control of Tribe could let Payward carry tokenized equity into private markets once the purchase closes. The report labeled that extension as its own reading of the deal rather than a stated Payward plan.

The announcement does not say which private-market products would launch first or when customers could access them, and it does not set out how Tribe's existing funds would sit inside the asset-management business after closing. The next verified markers are regulatory consents, the required closing conditions, and any Payward disclosure of the transaction price, none of which the company has published.
