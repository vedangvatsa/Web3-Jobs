Consensys Software Inc. plans to become two independently operated companies. The existing legal entity will continue under the MetaMask name and run the wallet and consumer products, while a newly formed Consensys will take the protocols group and institutional blockchain-infrastructure business, according to MetaMask's [Sept. 9 announcement](https://metamask.io/news/consensys-software-inc-to-become-two-independent-companies-metamask-and-consensys). The company expects the separation to complete by the end of 2026.

MetaMask says Joseph Lubin will be its chairman and chief executive. It says the new Consensys will be led by chief executive Mike Kriak and president David Cunningham, with Lubin as executive chairman. The [announcement](https://metamask.io/news/consensys-software-inc-to-become-two-independent-companies-metamask-and-consensys) names Linea, Besu, and Teku among the products and teams moving to the new Consensys.

MetaMask says no action is required from current users: their app, assets, keys, and access remain unchanged. It also says its SDKs, APIs, and developer tooling will continue, in the [announcement's FAQ](https://metamask.io/news/metamask-next-chapter). The companies will operate independently, while Consensys is to remain a channel partner for MetaMask; the release does not disclose the commercial terms of that relationship.

MetaMask frames its consumer strategy around self-custody. Its June [Money Account announcement](https://metamask.io/news/metamask-launches-money-account-bringing-defi-yield-and-global-spending-to-stablecoin-balances) says the product is built on Monad, uses the mUSD stablecoin, and combines automated earning, spending through the MetaMask Card where available, and trading. MetaMask says Money Account is not a bank account, savings account, or regulated investment product; it says mUSD balances are not insured by the FDIC or another government agency and that the stated APY is variable.

The company says Money Account is unavailable in the United Kingdom and other restricted jurisdictions in its [June announcement](https://metamask.io/news/metamask-launches-money-account-bringing-defi-yield-and-global-spending-to-stablecoin-balances). Users and developers will need to rely on current MetaMask product documentation for eligibility and any later change to product ownership, support, or terms.
