Network School is a startup society—a co-living, coworking, and community space designed for remote workers, founders, and creators. Located on an island off the coast of Singapore, it combines accommodation, meals, professional infrastructure, and a curated community of ambitious builders.

**Starting at $1,500/month** for shared accommodation or $3,000/month for a private room. Everything included.

## What You Get

**Living & Support**: Serviced rooms, healthy meals, high-speed WiFi, 24/7 gym.

**Workspace**: 24/7 coworking spaces, content studio for creators, makerspace for hardware projects.

**Community**: Regular speaker events, workshops, fitness classes, and networking with founders, operators, and builders across industries.

## Who's There

Remote workers, digital nomads, online creators, hardware founders, software founders, event organizers, engineers. The diversity is intentional—the best ideas come from unexpected combinations.

## The Bigger Picture

Network School isn't just a co-living space. The vision is to **build yourself up while building a startup society that bootstraps other startup societies**.

By joining, you're:
- Accessing world-class infrastructure and mentors
- Building relationships with ambitious peers
- Helping prove the model for future communities

## Location

Island near Singapore. Close to Southeast Asia's tech hub for investor access, meetings, and market opportunities. Far enough from urban distractions to actually focus.

## FAQ

**Cost?**
$1,500/month (shared room) or $3,000/month (private). Includes accommodation, meals, WiFi, gym, coworking, all facilities.

**How long can I stay?**
Memberships start monthly. Flexible duration, but longer commitments are prioritized.

**For tech founders only?**
No. Remote workers, creators, operators, organizers, engineers—anyone committed to growth and building.

**Why selective?**
To maintain community quality and cultural fit. Members share values of ambition, self-improvement, and techno-optimism.

**Where exactly?**
Island off the coast of Singapore. Close enough to the city for access, far enough to avoid distraction.

**Location advantage?**
Proximity to investor networks, emerging markets in Southeast Asia, favorable business environment and tax structure.
