## Onboarding Checklist for New Hires

This checklist is designed to guide new employees through their first few weeks, ensuring they have everything they need to succeed.

### Week 1: Foundation and Setup

- [ ] Complete all HR paperwork.
- [ ] Set up your computer, software, and all necessary accounts.
- [ ] Have an initial one-on-one with your direct manager to discuss expectations.
- [ ] Meet your immediate team members.
- [ ] Review the company's mission, vision, and organizational chart.
- [ ] Understand the team's primary communication channels (e.g., Slack, email).

### Week 2: Role Clarity and Integration

- [ ] Schedule introductory meetings with key collaborators outside your team.
- [ ] Clarify your role's key performance indicators (KPIs).
- [ ] Get access to all relevant project boards and documentation.
- [ ] Complete your first small, introductory task.
- [ ] Join all relevant recurring team meetings.

### First 30 Days: Learning and Contribution

- [ ] Understand the team's workflow and development cycle.
- [ ] Identify and review the key documents and resources for your role.
- [ ] Contribute to your first significant project.
- [ ] Have a 30-day check-in with your manager to review progress and ask questions.

### First 90 Days: Autonomy and Growth

- [ ] Begin to work more autonomously on your core responsibilities.
- [ ] Identify a mentor within the company.
- [ ] Start contributing ideas and suggestions in team discussions.
- [ ] Create a professional development plan with your manager.

### FAQs

**Q: Who is responsible for managing this checklist?**
A: While managers should guide the process, new hires should take ownership of their onboarding by proactively working through this checklist.

**Q: What if my company doesn't have a formal onboarding process?**
A: You can use this checklist as a guide to create your own onboarding plan, ensuring you get the information and access you need.

A structured onboarding process benefits both the employee and the company, leading to faster integration and higher job satisfaction.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Rushing the Process** - Don't expect overnight results. Sustainable change takes time.

2. **Ignoring Feedback** - Your colleagues, managers, and mentors see things you might miss. Listen to their input.

3. **One-Size-Fits-All Approach** - What works for someone else might not work for you. Adapt these strategies to your context.

4. **Giving Up Too Soon** - Change is uncomfortable. Push through the initial discomfort to reach better outcomes.

5. **Not Tracking Progress** - You can't improve what you don't measure. Keep metrics on your progress.
