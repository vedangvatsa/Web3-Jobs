Polymarket CEO Shayne Coplan [announced on September 10](https://x.com/shayne_coplan/status/2098068018949415250) that Warren Jenson had joined the company as its first chief financial officer.

Coplan's post says Jenson previously served as CFO of Amazon, Electronic Arts and Delta, and as president and CFO of Nielsen. It does not describe Jenson's responsibilities at Polymarket, the reason for the hire, or a regulatory or expansion strategy.
