Grvt, a hybrid exchange running on its own ZKsync-based Ethereum layer 2, launched Stable Funding Perps on Nov. 17, 2026. The new perpetual contracts open with seven commodity markets and set funding from what market makers pay to finance positions in traditional markets rather than from the gap between the perp price and an index.

The initial markets are gold, silver, platinum, palladium, WTI crude, Brent crude and natural gas, [Crypto Briefing reported](https://cryptobriefing.com/grvt-launches-stable-funding-perps-rwa/). Funding settles in USDC once each weekday at market close. Long and short positions carry separate rates, and the platform adjusts for holidays and weekend financing, with a triple charge on one weekday to cover the weekend.

On a conventional crypto perp, funding moves between longs and short holders every few hours based on how far the contract trades from the index. Grvt's model instead takes financing and carry rates from liquidity providers who hedge in traditional venues. Those rates, plus a platform adjustment, produce the funding number charged on positions held through the daily cut-off, [Finbold reported](https://finbold.com/grvt-launches-commodity-perpetuals-with-funding-tied-to-traditional-market-financing/).

Execution runs on a request-for-quote model. Market makers submit buy and sell quotes, manage their hedges outside crypto, and decide which quotes to accept, including through last-look controls. Grvt charges no trading fee; costs sit inside the quoted spread, with funding billed separately. Typical annual funding rates ran from about 4% into the high single digits in the product research materials, according to [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/grvt-launches-stable-funding-perps-rwa/). Actual execution costs and available liquidity vary with market conditions, order size and the quotes on offer.

Position limits reach 20 times on most markets, with silver capped at 10 times and lower caps on larger positions, the same report said. Contracts require multi-asset margin mode and USDC. Borrowed balances accrue hourly interest on top of funding.

Trading hours follow the underlying commodity venues rather than a crypto calendar. Most contracts run about 23 hours a day, five days a week, on the CME Globex schedule, and positions cannot be closed or reduced while the reference market is shut, Finbold [wrote](https://finbold.com/grvt-launches-commodity-perpetuals-with-funding-tied-to-traditional-market-financing/). Minimum trade sizes are larger at launch because of the liquidity sources behind the quotes. Grvt said it plans smaller minimums, more liquidity providers and broader hours over time.

Grvt co-founder and CEO Hong Yea said the structure is meant to keep outcomes tied to the underlying market. "If you're trading gold, oil or equities, your returns should primarily reflect your view of the underlying market, not unpredictable funding costs or unnecessary trading fees," he said, quoted by Finbold. Hong added that market makers which hedge efficiently in established markets can pass some of that through in pricing: "We don't believe every onchain market needs to recreate liquidity that already exists elsewhere."

In a June 2026 survey of 134 traders run by Grvt, 56% said they had skipped or closed a perpetual position because funding costs were unpredictable, the Finbold report said. The company also cited BitMEX research on funding-rate gaps across venues during the second quarter of 2026.

Grvt already lists classic perpetuals on commodities such as gold, silver and Brent oil. Stable Funding Perps add a second funding model rather than a new asset class, [Crypto Briefing noted](https://cryptobriefing.com/grvt-launches-stable-funding-perps-rwa/). The exchange operates a self-custodial trading stack on its custom ZKsync-based layer 2.

The roadmap extends beyond the first seven markets. Grvt aims to support more than 100 markets over time, including equities and other real-world assets, with a path toward 24/7 availability, [Finbold reported](https://finbold.com/grvt-launches-commodity-perpetuals-with-funding-tied-to-traditional-market-financing/). Real-world-asset perpetual open interest across venues reached about $18.46 billion by mid-October 2026, according to Crypto Briefing's market background.

Grvt's risk disclosure warns that leveraged trading carries liquidation and loss-of-principal risk, and that self-custody does not remove market, smart-contract or operational risks. The company also notes that makers can revise indicative funding rates intraday, so the rate shown at entry is not guaranteed for the life of the position.
