Standard Chartered analysts project that Japan's push into tokenized government and corporate bonds could serve as a major catalyst for the global real-world asset (RWA) market, helping drive total tokenized asset value toward $2 trillion.

Japan has established one of the clearest regulatory frameworks for security tokens and tokenized debt, enabling major domestic banks and brokerages to issue and settle fixed-income products onchain. According to the bank's research, institutional appetite for programmable settlement, fractionalized bond access, and automated coupon distribution is accelerating adoption across Asia-Pacific capital markets.

Analysts noted that sovereign and investment-grade corporate debt represent the largest addressable pool for onchain migration, outpacing earlier RWA experiments in private credit and real estate.
