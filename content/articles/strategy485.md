Strategy added 485 bitcoin for $33.4 million in the week to Nov. 15, lifting its holdings to 850,849 BTC, according to the company's [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000119312526462151/mstr-20261116.htm) on Monday. The purchase averaged $68,887 a coin, and the company has paid $64.20 billion for its stack, an average of $75,452, the filing shows.

The bitcoin buy was small next to the buyback. Strategy spent $228.1 million repurchasing 2,282,510 shares of Stretch (STRC), its variable-rate perpetual preferred stock, during the same week, nearly seven times what it put into bitcoin, [Unchained reported](https://unchainedcrypto.com/strategy-buys-485-bitcoin-for-33-4-million-lifting-holdings-to-850849-btc/). Both moves were paid for out of common stock sales: Strategy sold 2,087,040 MSTR shares for $244.7 million in net proceeds, sending $206.3 million to the buyback, $33.4 million to bitcoin and $5.0 million to cash. Interest on its cash and short-term investments covered another $21.8 million of the repurchase, the filing states. The company sold no preferred shares during the week.

Across its last three weekly filings, Strategy has spent $624.6 million buying back STRC and $180.8 million on bitcoin, by Unchained's tally of the 8-K reports. This week's 485 BTC was the smallest of the three purchases, after 811 BTC in the week ending Nov. 1 and 1,036 BTC in the week ending Nov. 8. Preferred buybacks came to $208.2 million and $188.3 million in those two weeks.

The repurchase program is running down. The filing says $332.2 million remains under the digital credit securities buyback authorization, and [Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/strategy-buys-485-bitcoin-for-33-4-million-lifting-holdings-to-850849-btc/) put the figure at $748.6 million as of Nov. 1. A separate $1.0 billion program for repurchasing MSTR shares went unused again.

Strategy also fixed its STRC dividend rate for next month. The board set the annual rate at 12.00% for STRC shares with record dates from Dec. 1 through Dec. 31, the filing said, and declared daily STRC dividends for December totaling $1.00 a share. Management will recommend that the board hold the rate there until STRC has shown what the filing calls "sustained, healthy trading" near $100 a share, Unchained reported. Quarterly dividends payable Dec. 31 were set at $2.50 a share on Strife (STRF) and Stride (STRD), $2.00 on Strike (STRK) and €2.50 on Stream (STRE), and the company expects the December payouts to count as non-taxable returns of capital for U.S. federal income tax purposes up to a holder's tax basis, the filing says.

The company drew $18.9 million from its USD Reserve during the week to help pay preferred dividends. That reserve stood at $4.75 billion on Nov. 15, with a further $151.2 million held as USD Cash.

Holdings keep climbing even as the buying slows. Strategy's stack is now roughly 4% of bitcoin's total supply, [Crypto Briefing reported](https://cryptobriefing.com/saylor-strategy-largest-company-bitcoin/), and the firm has signalled it could issue $10 billion to $30 billion more in what it calls digital credit instruments, supported by its asset base without requiring bitcoin to rise. Executive Chairman Michael Saylor has said the company aims to become the largest firm on the planet by market value, with bitcoin as the engine of that plan.

The purchases are funded by an equity machine that has already paid out heavily: Strategy has paid about $1.48 billion in cumulative dividends on its preferred shares through October 2026, according to Crypto Briefing. Those obligations recur whether bitcoin rallies or slides, the report said, and the company's cash reserves exist partly to cover them. Strategy reached a record market capitalization of nearly $128 billion in July 2025, and its market value passed that of Block Inc. in September 2026, the outlet reported.
