Strive bought 3,311 bitcoin between Oct. 5 and Oct. 9 for roughly $280 million, its largest purchase since January, lifting its treasury to 32,773 BTC. The Nasdaq-listed company [disclosed](https://decrypt.co/380858/strive-buys-280-million-bitcoin-largest-purchase-months) the buy in a filing on Monday, saying it paid an average of about $84,500 per coin including fees. The purchase took its stack from 29,462 BTC on Oct. 2 to 32,773 BTC a week later, worth roughly $2.73 billion at current prices.

The buy caps a stretch of heavy accumulation. Strive added $169 million in bitcoin earlier this month in what was then its largest buy in four months, after pushing holdings past 27,000 BTC with a $94.5 million purchase in late September, [Decrypt reported](https://decrypt.co/380858/strive-buys-280-million-bitcoin-largest-purchase-months). The company, which trades as ASST, went public through a merger with Asset Entities and recently completed a tie-up with Semler Scientific to bulk up its balance sheet. Beyond bitcoin it holds about 505,000 shares of Strategy's STRC preferred stock, valued near $50 million, and roughly $310 million in cash.

The timing stands out. Strive bought as bitcoin slid through a rough week, picking up coins at an average price well below recent highs near $87,000. At last look on Tuesday bitcoin traded at roughly $83,300 while ASST shares sat near $27.52, [Cointelegraph reported](https://cointelegraph.com/news/strive-bitcoin-holdings-32773-btc-warrant-exercises). The latest purchases raised bitcoin per share even as the company issued more equity: reserves grew 11.2% over the reporting period against a 9.4% rise in the effective common share count.

Warrant exercises funded part of the expansion. Holders had exercised $208 million worth of traditional warrants ahead of their 5 p.m. ET expiry on Tuesday, CEO Matt Cole [wrote](https://unchainedcrypto.com/strive-adds-3311-bitcoin-as-warrants-covering-15-3-million-shares-near-expiry/) on X, with about 15.3 million shares underlying the remaining warrants. The Oct. 13 filing shows the shares underlying the warrants falling by just under 8 million in the week to Oct. 9, to 15,255,900, while Class A shares outstanding grew by about 9.46 million, [Unchained reported](https://unchainedcrypto.com/strive-adds-3311-bitcoin-as-warrants-covering-15-3-million-shares-near-expiry/). On Sept. 2 Cole had said more than $700 million of warrants were set to expire in mid-October, with the proceeds available for more bitcoin if holders exercised them.

Strive also tightened the terms of its Variable Rate Series A Perpetual Preferred Stock, known as SATA. In a second filing the company said it amended the stock's terms with the Nevada Secretary of State so it cannot sell new SATA shares below $100 each before discounts and fees, making a previously announced policy binding, [Unchained reported](https://unchainedcrypto.com/strive-adds-3311-bitcoin-as-warrants-covering-15-3-million-shares-near-expiry/). The same amendment redefined a business day to include weekends, so SATA could pay daily dividends on Saturdays and Sundays where clearing allows, though Strive said it does not expect its near-term dividend cadence to change. The board held SATA's annual dividend rate at 13.00% for periods starting Nov. 1 and declared a daily cash dividend of $0.0571 a share for each of November's 19 business days.

The purchase makes Strive the fifth-largest publicly traded corporate bitcoin holder, according to industry [data](https://cointelegraph.com/news/strive-bitcoin-holdings-32773-btc-warrant-exercises) cited by Cointelegraph. Michael Saylor praised the strategy on X, writing that Strive is turning demand for its equity and preferred shares into demand for bitcoin. Strategy itself added a comparatively modest 250 BTC last week, bringing its total to 848,250 BTC and keeping its place as the largest public corporate holder, [Cointelegraph noted](https://cointelegraph.com/news/strive-bitcoin-holdings-32773-btc-warrant-exercises). The filing leaves some gaps. Neither it nor Cole's post says over what period the $208 million in exercises arrived, and no final tally had been published as of Tuesday, [Unchained noted](https://unchainedcrypto.com/strive-adds-3311-bitcoin-as-warrants-covering-15-3-million-shares-near-expiry/). SATA shares traded at $99.40 at last look, just under the new $100 floor. The wider treasury sector stayed busy alongside Strive: Strategy added $21.5 million in bitcoin while buying back preferred stock, and Bitmine kept stacking ether even as its chairman said purchases will stop once it holds 5% of the supply, [Decrypt reported](https://decrypt.co/380858/strive-buys-280-million-bitcoin-largest-purchase-months). SATA shares outstanding rose by 532,094 to 14,030,176 in the week to Oct. 9.
