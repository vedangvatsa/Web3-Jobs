## Overview

The **Super Hackathon** is a premier, month‑long development sprint hosted on the **opBNB** network. It brings together developers, designers, and entrepreneurs to build innovative decentralized applications (dApps) that showcase the power of **BNB Chain** and its Layer‑2 scaling solution.

### Why Participate?

* **Earn up to $20,000 USDT** in prize money across multiple categories (Best UI/UX, Most Innovative Use‑Case, Highest On‑Chain Impact, etc.).
* **Gain exposure** to top Web3 investors, venture studios, and strategic partners such as **Google Cloud**, **Chainlink**, and **Polygon**.
* **Accelerate your product** with mentorship from seasoned builders, technical support from the opBNB team, and access to free testnet credits.
* **Join a global community** of over 5,000 participants from 70+ countries, fostering collaboration and networking opportunities.

### How It Works

1. **Register** – Sign up on the official portal (link below) and receive a unique API key for the opBNB testnet.
2. **Build** – You have 30 days to develop a functional dApp. Use any stack (Solidity, Rust, JavaScript) and integrate opBNB’s fast, low‑cost transactions.
3. **Submit** – Upload your code repository, demo video, and a brief pitch deck.
4. **Demo Day** – Present live to a panel of judges and a global audience. Winners are announced on the final day.
