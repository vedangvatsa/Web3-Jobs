### Other Top Web3 Job Boards

#### 1. CryptoJobsList

CryptoJobsList is one of the oldest and most recognized **job crypto** boards, featuring a vast number of listings with a global reach.

- **Pros:** Offers a high volume of job postings, suitable for both technical and non-technical roles, and enjoys strong brand recognition. Many **Web3 jobs remote** listings are available here.
- **Cons:** The sheer number of listings can be overwhelming, and it may contain more low-quality or spammy postings than more curated platforms.

#### 2. Web3.career

Web3.career features a clean interface with a strong emphasis on technical roles. It provides excellent filtering options and a solid list of **crypto companies hiring**.

- **Pros:** User-friendly interface, strong focus on developer jobs, good filtering by tech stack. It's an excellent source for **web developer vacancies**.
- **Cons:** Offers fewer non-technical roles compared to other boards.

#### 3. Bankless "Work in Crypto" Board

Operated by the well-known Web3 media company Bankless, this job board is part of their broader ecosystem.

- **Pros:** High-quality listings from reputable companies within the Bankless network. The "Talent Collective" feature allows job seekers to create profiles for companies to discover.
- **Cons:** Smaller volume of listings compared to larger aggregators.

### Niche and Specialized Platforms

- **ETHGlobal Jobs:** After participating in an ETHGlobal hackathon, you can access their job board featuring roles from sponsoring companies. This platform offers high-quality opportunities in the **job in cryptocurrency** space.
- **The Graph Talent:** For developers skilled in building subgraphs, The Graph maintains a list of bounties and job opportunities, making it ideal for finding **cryptography job opportunities**.
- **DAO-Specific Job Boards:** Many large DAOs, such as Aave, Lido, and MakerDAO, have their own job boards or "contribute" pages accessible via their governance forums. If you're targeting a specific protocol, check their website for job openings. You might also find **crypto jobs remote no experience** roles here.
- **Reddit:** Subreddits like r/CryptoJobs, along with specific project subreddits, can yield valuable job leads, leading to the phrase "**Web3 jobs Reddit**".
