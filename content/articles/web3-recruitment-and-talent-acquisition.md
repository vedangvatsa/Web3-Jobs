Recruiting in Web3 is unlike recruiting in any other industry. The talent is scarce, the competition is fierce, and the best candidates are often pseudonymous contributors who are motivated by factors far beyond a simple salary. Traditional recruiting methods are woefully inadequate for finding and attracting elite Web3 talent. This guide provides actionable strategies for recruiting in the Web3 space.
