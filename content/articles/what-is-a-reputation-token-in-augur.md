## What is a Reputation Token (REP) in Augur?

**Reputation (REP)** is the native ERC-20 token of the Augur protocol. Unlike many other crypto tokens that are used for payments or governance voting on protocol parameters, REP has a very specific and unique function: **it is used by its holders to report on the outcomes of prediction markets**.

In essence, REP is a "reporter" token. The entire security of Augur's decentralized oracle rests on the economic incentives created around REP.

### The Purpose of REP: Securing the Oracle

The biggest challenge for a decentralized prediction market is determining the true outcome of a real-world event without relying on a centralized source. Augur solves this with a crypto-economic game theory system where REP holders are the referees.

Here's how the process works:

1.  **Staking for Reporting**: After a prediction market ends, it enters a reporting phase. REP holders can stake some of their REP on what they believe the true outcome was.
2.  **Incentive for Honesty**: If a reporter stakes their REP on the outcome that ultimately becomes the consensus, they are rewarded with a portion of the protocol's fees. They earn a return on their staked REP for doing their "job" correctly.
3.  **Penalty for Dishonesty**: If a reporter stakes their REP on an outcome that is successfully disputed and overturned, they lose their entire stake. This creates a strong financial disincentive to report untruthfully.

### The Dispute Mechanism

What if there is disagreement on the outcome? This is where the power of REP becomes most apparent.

-   **Challenging an Outcome**: Any REP holder can challenge a reported outcome by staking their REP on a different outcome. This challenge bond must be larger than the previous stake.
-   **Escalating Dispute Rounds**: This kicks off a 7-day dispute window where other REP holders can "pile on" by staking on one side or the other. If enough REP is staked on the challenging outcome, it becomes the new tentative outcome.
-   **Forking the Universe**: This can continue for several rounds. In the extremely rare and contentious event that a dispute becomes large enough (involving a significant percentage of the entire REP supply), it can trigger a "fork." In this scenario, the Augur universe itself splits into two versions—one where Outcome A is true and one where Outcome B is true. REP holders must then migrate their tokens to the universe they believe reflects reality. The universe that the market majority chooses becomes the canonical one, and the REP in the dishonest universe becomes worthless.

### Key Characteristics of REP

-   **Not a Governance Token (for parameters)**: REP is not used to vote on things like protocol upgrades or changing fees. Its sole purpose is to participate in the reporting and dispute process.
-   **A Right and a Responsibility**: Holding REP gives you the right to earn fees by reporting on outcomes, but it also comes with the responsibility to participate honestly in the system. A passive REP holder earns no rewards.
-   **The Value Proposition**: The value of the REP token is theoretically tied to the value of the fees generated by the Augur protocol. The more trading activity on Augur, the more fees are available to be earned by honest reporters, making REP more valuable to hold.

REP is a pioneering example of using crypto-economic incentives to solve a complex coordination problem, creating a decentralized system for arriving at "truth."
