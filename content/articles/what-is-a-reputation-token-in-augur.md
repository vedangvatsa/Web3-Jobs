### Introduction

In the ecosystem of the decentralized prediction market protocol Augur, the Reputation token (REP) plays a unique and highly specialized role. Unlike most crypto tokens, REP is not designed to be a medium of exchange or a store of value. Its sole purpose is to serve as a tool within Augur's decentralized oracle system. Holders of REP are financially incentivized to report the true outcomes of real-world events, forming the crypto-economic backbone that allows the protocol to function without a central authority.

### How REP Works: The Oracle Mechanism

The value and function of REP are tied directly to the process of resolving prediction markets on Augur.

1.  **Reporting on Outcomes**: After a prediction market's event has concluded, the market enters a "reporting" phase. Any holder of REP can participate by staking some of their REP tokens on what they believe was the true outcome of the event. For example, in a market asking "Will Team A win the championship?", a REP holder would stake their REP on either "Yes" or "No."

2.  **Incentivizing Truthfulness**: This is the core of the game theory behind REP.
    -   Reporters who stake their REP on the outcome that ultimately becomes the consensus "truth" are **rewarded**. They receive a pro-rata share of the fees generated by that market.
    -   Reporters who stake their REP on an outcome that is determined to be false **lose their staked REP**. This creates a direct financial penalty for dishonesty or negligence.

3.  **Disputing Incorrect Outcomes**: If an initial reporter is dishonest or mistaken, any other REP holder can challenge their report by staking a larger amount of REP on a different outcome. This triggers a dispute round.
    -   The dispute process can escalate through several rounds, with an increasing amount of REP required to be staked each time.
    -   If a dispute escalates far enough, it can trigger a "fork," where the entire network of REP holders is called upon to vote. The chain of events (and the version of REP) that the majority votes for is considered the canonical truth, and the other chain is abandoned.

4.  **A Work-Token, Not a Governance Token**: It is critical to understand that REP is a "work token." Its holders have the right and responsibility to perform the work of reporting for the network. It does not grant its holders the right to vote on protocol upgrades or changes to the Augur smart contracts themselves. That is a separate governance function.

### Why It Matters

The REP token and its associated oracle system are what make Augur a truly decentralized protocol.

-   **Trustless Resolution**: The system does not rely on a trusted third party or a centralized API to determine the truth. It relies on a decentralized network of economically incentivized individuals. The assumption is that the majority of REP holders will act honestly to protect the long-term value of their holdings and earn fees, as the value of REP is intrinsically linked to the integrity of the oracle it powers.
-   **Censorship Resistance**: Because anyone with REP can report on any market, it is extremely difficult for any party to censor an outcome or force an incorrect resolution.
-   **Skin in the Game**: The requirement to stake REP to report or dispute ensures that participants have "skin in thegame." They have a financial stake in the honesty and accuracy of the network.

### The Value of REP

The theoretical value of the REP token is derived from the work it enables its holders to do. The total value of all REP tokens (its market capitalization) should, in theory, be high enough to make it economically infeasible for an attacker to corrupt the oracle. An attacker would need to acquire more than 50% of all staked REP to guarantee the outcome of a disputed market, an expensive undertaking. The value of REP is therefore a function of:

-   The total value of all open interest on Augur's markets.
-   The fees generated by the platform, which are distributed to honest reporters.
-   The market's belief in the long-term viability and security of the protocol.

### FAQ

**Do I need REP to trade on Augur?**
No. Trading on Augur markets is done with ETH or a stablecoin like DAI. You only need REP if you want to participate in the market resolution process by reporting on outcomes or disputing them.

**What is the difference between REP and REPv2?**
Augur has gone through several upgrades. REPv2 is the current version of the token. The "v2" signifies that it is associated with the second major version of the Augur protocol. Holders of the original REP token were able to migrate their tokens to REPv2.

**Is holding REP risky?**
Yes. In addition to the normal price volatility of any crypto asset, holding REP carries the additional risk of losing your tokens if you participate in reporting and stake on an outcome that is ultimately deemed incorrect by the network. It is a token for active participants who are willing to do the work of securing the oracle.
