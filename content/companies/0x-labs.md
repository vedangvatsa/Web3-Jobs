0x Labs is known for its work in the decentralized finance (DeFi) space, specifically in creating protocols for decentralized exchanges. The company focuses on enabling peer-to-peer trading of tokens on the Ethereum blockchain and other networks. 

Their flagship product, the 0x Protocol, allows developers to build decentralized exchanges and trading applications. This protocol enables the exchange of tokens without the need for a centralized intermediary. 

0x Labs also contributes to the development of various tools and resources for developers in the blockchain ecosystem. The company is actively involved in the growth of the DeFi sector, making it an attractive place for professionals interested in blockchain technology and decentralized applications.
