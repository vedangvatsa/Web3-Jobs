0x provides a framework for building decentralized exchanges (DEXs) on the Ethereum blockchain. It enables peer-to-peer trading of tokens without the need for a centralized intermediary. The protocol utilizes smart contracts to facilitate trades, ensuring security and transparency.

The 0x protocol includes features such as order relaying and settlement, allowing developers to create their own DEXs or integrate decentralized trading into existing applications. 0x has a focus on interoperability, enabling different DEXs to communicate and share liquidity.
