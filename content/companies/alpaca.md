Alpaca is an API-first brokerage platform designed to enable developers, businesses, and traders to build and scale investing applications. While initially focused on U.S. equities, Alpaca has significantly expanded its infrastructure to include cryptocurrency trading.

## Developer Ecosystem
The platform offers a unified API that allows fintechs and developers to integrate smooth crypto and stock trading into their own apps. Alpaca handles the complex backend requirements of brokerage operations, including execution, clearing, custody, and compliance, making it a critical infrastructure layer for modern financial applications.
