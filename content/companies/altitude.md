Altitude is a global business account and financial operating system built on modern stablecoin rails, accessible from 200+ countries. Developed by Squads, Altitude provides companies with USD and EUR business accounts without requiring a US entity, corporate Visa cards with up to 2% cashback, global payments with multi-currency FX, automated bill pay, invoicing, and treasury management.

### Global Business Accounts & Payments
Altitude allows international companies to open USD and EUR accounts, receive client payments via USDC, ACH, and wire, and pay global teams and vendors from a single operating account.

### Programmable Controls & Security
Built on Squads' self-custodial smart account infrastructure, every account is controlled directly by the organization. Teams can configure granular member permissions, spending limits, and multi-signature approval thresholds for corporate operations.
