Anchorage Digital is a digital asset platform that provides institutional custody, trading, and financing services for cryptocurrencies and digital assets.

## Company Overview

Anchorage Digital Bank, N.A. received conditional approval from the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) in January 2021 to become the first federally chartered digital asset bank in the United States. This landmark approval allowed the company to provide banking services specifically for digital assets under federal regulation.

## Products and Services

Anchorage Digital offers a detailed suite of services for institutional clients:

- **Digital Asset Custody**: Secure storage solutions for cryptocurrencies and tokens
- **Trading Services**: Access to liquidity and trading capabilities
- **Staking Services**: Participation in proof-of-stake networks
- **Governance**: Tools for participating in blockchain governance
- **Settlement Services**: Efficient settlement of digital asset transactions
- **Integration**: APIs and infrastructure for institutional workflows

## Customers

Anchorage Digital serves institutional clients including:
- Asset managers
- Hedge funds
- Banks and financial institutions
- Corporations
- High net worth individuals
- DAOs and crypto-native organizations

## Security and Compliance

As a federally chartered bank, Anchorage Digital operates under strict regulatory oversight and maintains strong security practices. The company employs advanced cryptographic techniques and institutional-grade security measures to protect client assets.

## Technology

Anchorage Digital has developed proprietary technology including multi-party computation (MPC) and secure enclave technology to enable secure digital asset custody without traditional single points of failure.
