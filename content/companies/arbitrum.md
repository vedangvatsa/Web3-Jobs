Arbitrum enhances Ethereum's transaction throughput and reduces fees through its rollup technology. Built by Offchain Labs, the Arbitrum ecosystem includes Arbitrum One, Arbitrum Nova, and the Arbitrum Orbit framework for custom chains. It allows developers to deploy Ethereum-compatible smart contracts while benefiting from increased speed and lower costs.

## Products and Services

- **Arbitrum One**: Optimistic rollup for general-purpose smart contracts
- **Arbitrum Nova**: Ultra-low-cost chain for high-volume applications
- **Arbitrum Orbit**: Framework for launching custom L3 chains
- **Stylus**: Write smart contracts in Rust, C, and C++

Working at Arbitrum offers opportunities to contribute to a leading solution in the blockchain space. The team is actively hiring for key positions, focusing on scaling, ecosystem growth, and protocol security.
