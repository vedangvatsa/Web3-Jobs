Arcade is a non-custodial liquidity protocol enabling peer-to-peer and pooled lending against non-fungible tokens, digital collectibles, and tokenized real-world assets. By tokenizing loan terms into smart contracts via Arcade Protocol, users can unlock liquidity from high-value digital collateral without selling their underlying positions. Built with audited smart contract escrow architectures, Arcade bridges structured credit with decentralized ownership. The team values smart contract security, decentralized finance architecture, liquidity modeling, and clean UX.
