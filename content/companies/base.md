name: Base

description: Base is a platform for building decentralized applications.
---

Base is a layer-2 solution built on Ethereum, designed to provide developers with a secure and scalable environment for creating decentralized applications. The platform aims to enhance the user experience by offering lower transaction fees and faster processing times compared to the Ethereum mainnet.

Base supports various projects and initiatives within the Web3 ecosystem. The platform is integrated with popular Ethereum tools and wallets, making it easier for developers to transition their applications to a more efficient environment. Currently, Base has job listings for City Coordinators in multiple cities across the United States, indicating its expansion and community engagement efforts.
