Baton Corporation is the rapid-deployment software engineering lab and operating company behind pump.fun, the breakout crypto social network and token launchpad built on the Solana blockchain. Launched in early 2024, pump.fun became one of the fastest-growing and highest-revenue applications in Web3 history.

### About Baton Corporation
Baton Corporation engineers high-performance bonding curve algorithms, real-time streaming interfaces, and low-latency trading terminal infrastructure on Solana. Operating with an intense culture of shipping velocity and talent density, Baton Corporation builds consumer-facing on-chain social and financial products experienced by millions of users worldwide.
