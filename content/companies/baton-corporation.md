pump.fun (operated by Baton Corporation) is the breakout crypto social network and token launchpad built on the Solana blockchain. Launched in early 2024, pump.fun became one of the fastest-growing and highest-revenue applications in Web3 history.

### About pump.fun
pump.fun engineers high-performance bonding curve algorithms, real-time streaming interfaces, and low-latency trading terminal infrastructure on Solana. Operating with an intense culture of shipping velocity and talent density, the team builds consumer-facing on-chain social and financial products experienced by millions of users worldwide.
