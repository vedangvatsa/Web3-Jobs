Biconomy is a Web3 developer and transaction infrastructure platform that helps developers deliver smooth decentralized experiences. Founded in 2019, the company focuses on removing the UX friction associated with traditional blockchain interactions, such as gas fees and complex wallet management.

## Account Abstraction
Biconomy specializes in account abstraction (ERC-4337) and meta-transactions. By providing strong APIs and SDKs, they allow dApps to sponsor gas fees for their users, batch transactions, and simplify onboarding, making Web3 applications feel as intuitive as traditional Web2 products.

### About Biconomy
Biconomy is a leading organization in the Web3 Infrastructure & UX space, pioneering decentralized technologies, protocol security, and next-generation blockchain infrastructure. The company builds resilient products designed for global scalability, high performance, and trust-minimized user sovereignty.

Operating across international digital asset markets, Biconomy collaborates with developers, institutional partners, and ecosystem contributors to advance open-source software, decentralized finance, and distributed consensus. The organization fosters a high-impact engineering and product culture focused on shipping secure, user-centric Web3 applications, continuous technological innovation, and sustainable ecosystem growth.
