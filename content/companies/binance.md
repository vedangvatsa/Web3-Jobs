Binance is the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange by daily trading volume. Founded in 2017 by Changpeng Zhao (CZ), the platform supports trading of hundreds of cryptocurrencies and serves users globally.

## Products and Services

- **Spot Trading**: Buy and sell 350+ cryptocurrencies
- **Futures & Derivatives**: use trading products
- **Binance Earn**: Staking, savings, and yield products
- **BNB Chain**: Binance's native blockchain ecosystem (BNB Smart Chain and BNB Beacon Chain)
- **Binance Pay**: Cryptocurrency payment solution
- **Binance Academy**: Free educational platform for blockchain and crypto
- **Launchpad**: Token launch platform for new projects

## Technology

Binance operates its own blockchain ecosystem, BNB Chain, which hosts thousands of decentralized applications spanning DeFi, gaming, and NFTs. The exchange processes millions of transactions per second with its proprietary matching engine.
