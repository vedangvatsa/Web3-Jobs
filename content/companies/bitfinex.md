Professional cryptocurrency exchange and trading platform operated by iFinex Inc.

## Company Overview

Bitfinex is one of the longest-running cryptocurrency exchanges, founded in 2012. It offers advanced trading features including margin trading, lending, and derivatives. The platform supports trading of a wide variety of digital assets and is known for its deep liquidity. Bitfinex is operated by iFinex Inc, the same parent company as Tether, and played a key role in the early development of the crypto trading infrastructure.
