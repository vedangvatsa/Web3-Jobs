bitFlyer is one of Japan's largest and most established cryptocurrency exchanges, licensed by the JFSA and operating licensed trading services across Japan, the US, and Europe.

### About bitFlyer
bitFlyer is a leading organization in the Exchange space, pioneering decentralized technologies, protocol security, and next-generation blockchain infrastructure. The company builds resilient products designed for global scalability, high performance, and trust-minimized user sovereignty.

Operating across international digital asset markets, bitFlyer collaborates with developers, institutional partners, and ecosystem contributors to advance open-source software, decentralized finance, and distributed consensus. The organization fosters a high-impact engineering and product culture focused on shipping secure, user-centric Web3 applications, continuous technological innovation, and sustainable ecosystem growth.
