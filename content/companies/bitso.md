Bitso is a cryptocurrency exchange that enables the buying and selling of digital assets. It provides services for individuals and businesses, allowing users to trade various cryptocurrencies. The platform supports multiple fiat currencies, making it accessible for users in Latin America.

Bitso has been recognized for its compliance with regulatory standards in Mexico, where it is one of the leading exchanges. The company aims to promote the adoption of cryptocurrencies in the region by offering user-friendly services and educational resources. 

The company currently has job listings for various positions, including Associate General Counsel, Compliance Associate, and Product Manager. This indicates a focus on expanding its legal, compliance, and product development teams to support its growth in the cryptocurrency market.
