Braavos emphasizes security and ease of use, catering to both novice and experienced users in the Web3 space.

The company is currently seeking a Senior Full Stack Engineer to join their team. This role involves developing and maintaining the wallet's features and ensuring a high-quality user experience. Working at Braavos provides an opportunity to contribute to the evolving landscape of Web3 technology.
