## Company Overview

Brave is a privacy-first web browser that blocks ads and trackers by default. It features an integrated crypto wallet, the Basic Attention Token (BAT) reward system, and Brave Search. Founded by Brendan Eich, co-founder of Mozilla and creator of JavaScript.
