BTSE is a leading global cryptocurrency exchange and financial technology company. Founded in 2018, BTSE develops advanced trading and financial infrastructure that bridges traditional finance with digital assets, serving both individual retail traders and enterprise institutional clients worldwide.

## Company Overview

BTSE operates high-availability trading systems designed for deep liquidity, ultra-low latency order execution, and military-grade asset security. Alongside its core exchange platform, the BTSE Group provides comprehensive B2B enterprise fintech solutions, including turnkey white-label exchange software, OTC trading desks, crypto payment gateways, and asset management custody integrations.

## Products and Services

- **Spot and Derivatives Trading**: Multi-currency order books supporting major cryptocurrencies, stablecoins, and fiat currencies with deep market liquidity.
- **Enterprise Fintech & White-Label Exchange**: Turnkey exchange, brokerage, and liquidity infrastructure enabling financial institutions to launch regulated digital asset offerings.
- **BTSE Pay**: Cryptocurrency payment processing solutions allowing global e-commerce merchants to accept digital asset payments with automated instant settlement.
- **Institutional Custody & OTC**: High-touch institutional liquidity desk and secure multi-custody settlement for high-net-worth individuals and corporate treasuries.
