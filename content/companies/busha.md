Busha is a premier pan-African digital currency exchange and brokerage platform. Founded in 2018 by Michael Adeyeri and Chiefy Mbama, Busha became one of the first cryptocurrency exchanges in Africa to secure official provisional operating approval under the Nigerian Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) regulatory framework.

### About Busha
Busha offers user-friendly retail applications (Busha Consumer), merchant payment checkouts (Busha Commerce), and automated high-yield crypto savings (Busha Yield). Backed by prominent venture firms including Jump Capital, Busha is dedicated to building trustworthy, seamless, and compliant financial bridges for millions of Africans entering the digital economy.
