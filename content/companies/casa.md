Casa is a recognized leader in Bitcoin and multi-asset self-custody. Founded in 2016 by Nick Neuman and Jameson Lopp, Casa pioneered the multi-signature "multi-key" security model that eliminates single points of failure in private key storage for individuals, family offices, and institutions.

### About Casa
Casa provides users with multi-device cryptographic vaults where keys are distributed across mobile hardware, dedicated hardware wallets, and emergency recovery backups. With specialized features like Casa Inheritance and 24/7 client security support, Casa ensures digital wealth remains sovereign, resilient, and safe across generations.
