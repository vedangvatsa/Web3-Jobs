CertiK is a pioneer in blockchain security, utilizing advanced AI technology and expert manual review to protect and monitor blockchain protocols and smart contracts. Founded in 2018 by professors from Yale and Columbia University, CertiK is one of the most widely used and trusted security firms in the Web3 industry.

## Security Suite
The company offers a comprehensive suite of security services, including smart contract auditing, penetration testing, and continuous on-chain monitoring (Skynet). By employing rigorous formal verification methods, CertiK mathematically proves the correctness of smart contracts, helping to prevent exploits and ensure the safety of billions of dollars in digital assets.
