CEX.IO is one of the earliest and most respected international cryptocurrency exchanges, operating continuously since 2013. Founded by Oleksandr Lutskevych, CEX.IO has grown into a comprehensive digital asset ecosystem serving millions of registered users across the UK, Europe, Latin America, and globally.

### About CEX.IO
CEX.IO holds extensive regulatory licenses and registrations across multiple jurisdictions, including the UK, European Union, and United States. The platform provides institutional and retail customers with instant crypto purchases, spot and margin trading, custody solutions, staking yield, and payment card on/off-ramps with strict adherence to AML/KYC compliance.
