Chainalysis offers blockchain data and analysis services to government agencies, financial institutions, and cryptocurrency businesses. Their products help clients detect and prevent fraud, comply with regulations, and conduct investigations into illicit activities involving cryptocurrencies. 

The company is known for its software solutions that provide insights into blockchain transactions, enabling organizations to understand the flow of funds and identify suspicious activities. Chainalysis has a range of job openings, including positions in engineering, design, and client relations, reflecting its growth and the demand for skilled professionals in the blockchain analytics space.
