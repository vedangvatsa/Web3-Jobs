Circle is the principal operator of USDC, the second-largest stablecoin by market capitalization. USDC is backed 1:1 by U.S. dollar reserves and operates on 15+ blockchains.

## Products and Services

- **USDC**: Dollar-backed stablecoin
- **Circle Mint**: USDC minting and redemption for businesses
- **Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol (CCTP)**: Native USDC bridging
- **Programmable Wallets**: Embedded wallet infrastructure
