# About CKBA

CKBA (Common Knowledge Base Association) is a Swiss membership-based, non-profit association governed by its contributors, dedicated to supporting the development and growth of the CKB ecosystem.

## Coordination Layer for the CKB Ecosystem

CKBA serves as the coordination layer for the CKB ecosystem, advancing core protocol development, Layer 2 scaling infrastructure, and cryptographic innovations. Key ecosystem initiatives include Fiber Network, an open-source payment-channel network built on CKB enabling fast, low-cost, multi-hop payments.

## Governance & Community

Governed by its global contributors, CKBA aligns engineering scope, ecosystem grants, and developer tooling to drive real-world adoption of decentralized protocols and real-world utility across global Web3 markets.
