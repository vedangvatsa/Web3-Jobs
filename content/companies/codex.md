Codex is modern financial infrastructure for internet businesses, providing programmatic stablecoin settlement, automated treasury accounts, and global fiat-to-stablecoin rails. Headquartered in San Francisco, Codex replaces slow, fragmented cross-border banking rails with real-time, low-cost digital dollar transactions.

### About Codex
Codex allows software companies, marketplaces, and global enterprises to invoice, collect, and disburse digital dollars (USDC, USDT) worldwide with automated local bank off-ramps and enterprise accounting integrations, enabling frictionless global commerce.
