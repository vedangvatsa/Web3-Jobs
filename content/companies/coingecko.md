CoinGecko is a cryptocurrency data aggregator that provides real-time information on price, trading volume, and market capitalization for various cryptocurrencies. The platform offers a comprehensive suite of tools for users to track and analyze the performance of digital assets. CoinGecko also features a community section that includes news, events, and educational resources related to the cryptocurrency space.

The company operates GeckoTerminal, a product aimed at providing advanced trading tools and analytics for cryptocurrency traders. CoinGecko's user-friendly interface and extensive database make it a popular choice among cryptocurrency enthusiasts and investors. The company is known for its commitment to transparency and accessibility in the cryptocurrency market.
