CoinPoker is the flagship decentralized online poker and gaming platform powered by blockchain technology. Founded in 2017, CoinPoker replaces traditional black-box card shuffling with a provably fair, decentralized Random Number Generator (RNG) that allows players to verify every hand cryptographically.

### About CoinPoker
CoinPoker operates natively in major cryptocurrencies (including USDT, ETH, and BTC), enabling players worldwide to enjoy instant deposits, near-zero transaction fees, and rapid withdrawals without intermediary banking friction.
