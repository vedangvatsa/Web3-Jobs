CoinShares is Europe's largest and longest-standing digital asset investment and asset management firm. Founded in 2014 by Daniel Masters, Jean-Marie Mognetti, and Russell Newton, CoinShares pioneered European crypto investing by launching the world's first regulated Bitcoin exchange-traded product (ETP).

### About CoinShares
Publicly listed on Nasdaq Stockholm (ticker: CS), CoinShares provides institutional and retail investors with innovative digital asset investment products, physical crypto ETPs, algorithmic capital management, and macroeconomic digital asset research. With offices in Jersey, London, Paris, and New York, CoinShares sets the standard for regulated institutional digital asset access.
