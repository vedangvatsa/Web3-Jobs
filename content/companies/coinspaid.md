CoinsPaid (operating CryptoProcessing by CoinsPaid) is a leading cryptocurrency payment ecosystem and business financial infrastructure provider. Founded in 2014 by Max Krupyshev and headquartered in Tallinn, Estonia, CoinsPaid enables international online merchants to accept digital assets, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, and major stablecoins, with automated instant conversion and seamless settlement into fiat or crypto.

## Company Overview

CoinsPaid was established to bridge conventional commerce with decentralized finance. Over more than a decade of continuous operations, CoinsPaid has grown into one of the highest-volume crypto payment processors worldwide, serving hundreds of enterprise and online merchants globally. Its corporate and operational structure is anchored in Tallinn, Estonia, operating under strict European VASP compliance and regulatory supervision.

## Products and Services

- **CryptoProcessing by CoinsPaid**: Enterprise merchant payment gateway enabling businesses to accept 30+ cryptocurrencies with real-time exchange, automated volatility risk mitigation, and instant settlement.
- **Business Crypto Wallets**: Multi-currency corporate digital asset treasury management with automated recurring payments, segregated accounts, and multi-signature security.
- **Fiat Settlement Rails**: Direct integrations with European SEPA and SWIFT banking infrastructure allowing enterprise clients to convert incoming cryptocurrency deposits directly into EUR, USD, and other major fiat currencies.
- **Developer APIs & Plug-ins**: Plug-and-play e-commerce integrations, hosted checkout widgets, and robust REST APIs designed for high-throughput transactional environments.
