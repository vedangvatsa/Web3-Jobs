Algorithmic money market protocol for lending and borrowing crypto assets.

## Company Overview

Compound is a DeFi protocol that establishes money markets with algorithmically set interest rates based on supply and demand. Users can supply assets to earn interest or borrow against their collateral. Governed by COMP token holders.
