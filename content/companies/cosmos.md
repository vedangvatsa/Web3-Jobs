## Company Overview

Cosmos is an ecosystem of interconnected blockchains using the Inter-Blockchain Communication (IBC) protocol. The Cosmos SDK and Tendermint/CometBFT consensus engine power hundreds of application-specific chains including Osmosis, dYdX, and Injective.
