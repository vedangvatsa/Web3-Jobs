Crossmint offers a platform that enables businesses to create, manage, and sell NFTs. Their services focus on simplifying the NFT minting process for companies looking to incorporate digital assets into their offerings. Crossmint's technology allows for seamless integration of NFTs into existing workflows, catering to various industries.

The company currently has job openings for roles such as Senior Account Executive - Fintech, Sales Development Representative, and Full-stack Engineer - Payments. These positions are based in locations including New York City, Miami, and Spain. Working at Crossmint provides opportunities to engage with innovative technology in the NFT space and contribute to the growing adoption of digital assets in business.
