## Company Overview

Crypto.com is one of the world's largest cryptocurrency platforms, offering trading, DeFi services, a Visa card, NFT marketplace, and the Cronos blockchain. The company was founded in 2016 as Monaco and rebranded to Crypto.com in 2018.
