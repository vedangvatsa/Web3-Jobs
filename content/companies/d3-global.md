D3 Global is pioneering the DomainFi movement by bridging traditional internet naming infrastructure (DNS) and ICANN-accredited top-level domains with interoperable Web3 digital identities. Led by Fred Hsu, D3 collaborates directly with top-tier blockchain ecosystems and domain authorities to create officially recognized domain names that work seamlessly across browsers, crypto wallets, and messaging protocols.

### About D3 Global
D3 partners with major Layer-1 and Layer-2 networks—such as ApeChain, Shibarium, and Core DAO—to tokenize domain registries into authentic Web3 identities. By ensuring compatibility with legacy DNS root zones while unlocking on-chain verifiable domain ownership, D3 delivers identity infrastructure that reaches billions of internet and crypto users.
