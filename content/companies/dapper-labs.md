## Company Overview

Dapper Labs pioneered consumer-facing blockchain experiences with CryptoKitties (2017) and NBA Top Shot. They also built the Flow blockchain, a Layer 1 designed for NFTs and gaming with account abstraction and resource-oriented programming (Cadence).
