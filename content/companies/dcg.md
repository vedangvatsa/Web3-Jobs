Digital Currency Group (DCG) is a venture capital firm that invests in blockchain technology and digital currency companies. The firm has built a portfolio that includes a range of companies in the cryptocurrency ecosystem. DCG also owns Grayscale Investments, which offers cryptocurrency investment products.

DCG provides support to its portfolio companies through various services, including access to capital and strategic guidance. The firm is known for its active role in the blockchain industry, participating in initiatives that promote the growth of the digital currency ecosystem. Current job listings include positions for Associate and Research Associate in Liquid Tokens, indicating a focus on investment and research in this area.
