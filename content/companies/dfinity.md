The DFINITY Foundation is a not-for-profit scientific research organization based in Zurich, Switzerland, with research centers globally. Founded in 2016, DFINITY is the major contributor to the Internet Computer (ICP), a decentralized blockchain network designed to extend the functionality of the public internet.

## Internet Computer (ICP)
The Internet Computer aims to provide a "World Computer" that can host secure software, decentralized applications (dApps), and enterprise systems directly on-chain without relying on centralized cloud providers like AWS or Google Cloud. DFINITY's large team of cryptographers and distributed systems engineers continuously build core infrastructure and scaling solutions for the network.
