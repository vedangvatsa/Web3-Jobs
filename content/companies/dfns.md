DFNS is the digital asset operating platform for regulated finance, providing core banking infrastructure for digital assets. Powered by cryptographic Multi-Party Computation (MPC), DFNS enables banks, fintechs, and digital asset institutions to embed programmatic, non-custodial crypto wallets and key management directly into their applications.

### What They Build

- **Wallet-as-a-Service (WaaS)**: Developer-first APIs and SDKs allowing fintechs and Web3 apps to create and manage programmatic digital asset wallets across 100+ blockchain networks.
- **Multi-Party Computation (MPC) Key Management**: Distributed cryptographic key-share architecture that eliminates single points of failure, protecting billions in assets without taking custodial ownership.
- **Policy Engine & Governance**: Enterprise transaction approval workflows, granular role-based access control, biometric verification, and automated compliance policy enforcement.
- **Institutional Deployment**: SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified technology deployable across SaaS, hybrid, or on-premise environments with 99.997% historic uptime.

### Culture & Engineering

DFNS is a remote-first, globally distributed team founded in 2021 with headquarters in Paris and team hubs in New York, San Francisco, Geneva, Dubai, and Singapore. The team values security-first engineering, documentation clarity, and end-to-end ownership.
