Dialectic (often referred to as Dialectic Group) is a Swiss-based, crypto-native investment and building firm. Founded in 2020 by Ryan Zurrer, the firm operates as a multi-strategy enterprise that combines capital deployment with technological development in the Web3 and decentralized finance (DeFi) sectors.

## Core Operations
Dialectic functions as an asset manager and venture investor, focusing on providing risk-adjusted exposure to alternative assets, DeFi protocols, and blockchain infrastructure. They manage hundreds of millions in assets and have been involved in seed and strategic rounds for numerous prominent Web3 projects.

Beyond passive investment, Dialectic defines itself as a "crypto-native organization of builders." They develop proprietary tools, engage in on-chain strategies, and work to build financial infrastructure.
