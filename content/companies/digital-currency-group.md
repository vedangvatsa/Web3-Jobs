Crypto conglomerate and parent company of Grayscale, Foundry, and CoinDesk.

## Company Overview

Digital Currency Group (DCG) is a global enterprise building, buying, and investing in blockchain companies. Its subsidiaries include Grayscale Investments, Foundry, Luno, and it previously owned CoinDesk. Founded by Barry Silbert.

### About Digital Currency Group
Digital Currency Group is a leading organization in the Venture Capital / Conglomerate space, pioneering decentralized technologies, protocol security, and next-generation blockchain infrastructure.
