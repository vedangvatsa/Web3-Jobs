DV Trading is an established proprietary trading firm that expanded into the cryptocurrency space through its affiliate, DV Chain. DV Chain operates as a specialized OTC (Over-the-Counter) liquidity provider and market maker.

## Services
DV Chain focuses on serving institutional clients, exchanges, and token projects by offering 24/7 liquidity across a wide range of digital assets. They provide strong API connectivity and proprietary trading interfaces, enabling institutions to execute large trades efficiently with competitive pricing and reduced market friction.
