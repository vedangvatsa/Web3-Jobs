Ethereum Institutional is an initiative focused on positioning Ethereum as foundational financial infrastructure for global institutions.

The organization works directly with banks, asset managers, exchanges, and custodians to frame technical requirements, scope integration pathways, and deploy institutional solutions on Ethereum.
