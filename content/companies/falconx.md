FalconX is a leading digital asset prime broker and trading platform built specifically for institutional investors. Founded in 2018, the company provides a detailed suite of services that bridge traditional finance and digital assets.

## Core Operations
The platform offers deep liquidity, credit, and clearing services, enabling institutions to execute large block trades with minimal market impact. FalconX is known for its proprietary technology that aggregates liquidity from major exchanges and OTC desks, providing optimal pricing and smooth trade execution.
