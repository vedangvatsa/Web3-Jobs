Figment offers a suite of products designed to support blockchain networks and developers. Their services include staking, data services, and developer tools that facilitate the building and scaling of decentralized applications. Figment's staking services allow users to earn rewards by participating in various blockchain networks.

The company focuses on enhancing the accessibility and usability of blockchain technology. They aim to simplify the process for developers and enterprises looking to integrate blockchain solutions. Figment also provides educational resources to help users understand the benefits and functionalities of blockchain technology.
