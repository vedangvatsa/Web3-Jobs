Fireblocks provides digital asset infrastructure for exchanges, banks, lending desks, and hedge funds. The platform uses MPC-based wallet technology and has processed over $6 trillion in digital assets.

## Products and Services

- **MPC Wallets**: Multi-party computation wallet infrastructure
- **Fireblocks Network**: Secure asset transfer network
- **Tokenization**: Creating and managing tokenized assets
- **DeFi Access**: Secure institutional DeFi access
- **Web3 Engine**: APIs for building Web3 applications
