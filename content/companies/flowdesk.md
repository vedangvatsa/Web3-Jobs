Flowdesk is a full-service digital asset market maker and crypto trading infrastructure provider. Founded in Paris in 2020 by Guilhem Chaumont, Paul Michelet, François Cluzeau, and Balthazar Giraux, Flowdesk delivers algorithmic liquidity, OTC trading services, and programmatic execution for token projects, centralized exchanges, and decentralized liquidity pools.

### About Flowdesk
Flowdesk operates with a unique "Market Making as a Service" (MMaaS) model that gives token issuers full transparency, control, and ownership over their market making operations and liquidity strategies. Regulated as a Digital Asset Service Provider (DASP) in France by the AMF and expanding globally across Singapore, North America, and Europe, Flowdesk builds high-throughput low-latency execution engines bridging TradFi precision with decentralized liquidity dynamics.
