Galaxy Digital is a financial services and investment management firm focused on digital assets and blockchain technology. Founded by Mike Novogratz, it is publicly listed on the TSX (GLXY).

## Products and Services

- **Galaxy Trading**: Market-making and OTC trading
- **Galaxy Asset Management**: Digital asset fund management
- **Galaxy Ventures**: Venture capital investing in blockchain companies
- **Galaxy Mining**: Bitcoin mining and hosting
- **GK8**: Self-custody platform for institutions
