Gelato offers a decentralized network that automates smart contract executions on Ethereum and other blockchains. Their platform allows developers to schedule transactions, ensuring that decentralized applications can perform actions without manual intervention. This automation is crucial for applications that require timely execution, such as DeFi protocols and NFT platforms.

The company is known for its Gelato Network, which connects developers with reliable transaction execution services. Gelato's infrastructure supports various use cases, including automated trading and event-driven actions within smart contracts. They aim to enhance the user experience in decentralized applications by minimizing the need for users to monitor and execute transactions manually.

Gelato is actively hiring for various roles, indicating growth and the need for talent in areas such as security, talent acquisition, and product management. This suggests a dynamic work environment focused on innovation in the blockchain space.
