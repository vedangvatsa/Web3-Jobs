Gemini is a regulated cryptocurrency exchange and custodian founded by Tyler and Cameron Winklevoss. The platform is a New York Trust Company regulated by the NYDFS.

## Products and Services

- **Gemini Exchange**: Spot trading for 70+ cryptocurrencies
- **Gemini Custody**: Institutional digital asset custody
- **Gemini Dollar (GUSD)**: Regulated USD stablecoin
- **Gemini ActiveTrader**: Professional trading platform
