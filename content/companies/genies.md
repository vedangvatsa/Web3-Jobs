Genies is a technology company that specializes in creating personalized digital avatars. Their platform allows users to design and customize avatars that can be used across various digital environments, including social media and virtual worlds. The company focuses on enhancing user expression through these avatars, which can represent individuals in a variety of contexts.

The company has job listings for roles such as Senior Machine Learning Engineer and Technical Artist, indicating a strong emphasis on advanced technology and artistic design. Genies also offers contract positions, such as Influencer Partnerships Specialist and AI Character Developer, reflecting their engagement with influencers and the broader digital community. Their focus on innovation in avatar technology and partnerships with brands positions them as a notable player in the digital identity space.
