GSR (Global Social Risk) Markets is one of the oldest and most prominent market makers and algorithmic trading firms in the cryptocurrency industry. Founded in 2013 by former Goldman Sachs executives, GSR provides liquidity, risk management, and trading solutions to token issuers, exchanges, and institutional investors.

## Expertise
The firm specializes in providing continuous, two-sided liquidity across numerous trading pairs and venues. Beyond market making, GSR offers strategic OTC trading, treasury management for Web3 projects, and structured products, playing a critical role in maintaining the efficiency and stability of global crypto markets.
