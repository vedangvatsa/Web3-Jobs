Hedera provides a decentralized network for building and deploying applications. It uses a unique consensus algorithm called Hashgraph, which aims to offer fast transaction speeds and low fees. Hedera supports various use cases, including tokenization, decentralized finance, and supply chain management.

The platform is governed by the Hedera Governing Council, which consists of leading organizations across multiple industries. Hedera's technology is designed to be secure and scalable, making it suitable for enterprise applications. The company currently has job listings for positions such as Sales Director and Senior DevOps Engineer, indicating a focus on expanding its team and capabilities.
