Horizen Labs builds enterprise-ready modular blockchain architecture and zero-knowledge cryptographic systems. Founded by Rob Viglione and team, Horizen Labs originally contributed to the Horizen ecosystem and has engineered foundational Web3 infrastructure, including ApeChain, custom zero-knowledge verification layers, and modular Layer-3 rollup ecosystems.

### About Horizen Labs
Horizen Labs focuses on making zero-knowledge technology and modular execution accessible to enterprises, gaming studios, and decentralized communities. With deep expertise across EVM compatibility, ZK-SNARKs, and high-performance consensus networks, Horizen Labs empowers protocol developers to launch fast, secure, and customizable on-chain environments.
