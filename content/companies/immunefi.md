Immunefi is the premier bug bounty and security services platform designed exclusively for the Web3 ecosystem. It connects smart contract developers and blockchain projects with a global community of elite ethical hackers and security researchers.

## Bug Bounties
By offering substantial financial rewards for responsibly disclosing vulnerabilities, Immunefi actively prevents critical exploits before they occur. The platform has paid out millions of dollars in bounties and is trusted by leading DeFi protocols, exchanges, and infrastructure providers to secure their ecosystems against catastrophic hacks.
