Index Industries is a Web3 financial engineering lab developing decentralized asset management primitives, algorithmic on-chain yield engines, and automated smart contract structured products for modern decentralized finance ecosystems.

### About Index Industries
Index Industries focuses on non-custodial capital efficiency, developing composable financial primitives that allow liquidity providers and protocols to generate sustainable yield, manage automated risk tranches, and construct diversified on-chain portfolios with mathematical precision.
