Injective Labs is known for creating Injective Protocol, a decentralized exchange that supports various financial products, including derivatives. The platform allows users to trade with zero gas fees and offers a fully decentralized order book. Injective Protocol aims to provide a seamless trading experience while ensuring high liquidity and security.

The company focuses on building infrastructure that enables developers to create and launch their own decentralized applications (dApps) on the Injective Protocol. They emphasize scalability and interoperability, allowing users to access multiple blockchain networks. Injective Labs is actively hiring for roles such as Infrastructure Engineer, indicating growth and the need for skilled professionals in the blockchain space.
