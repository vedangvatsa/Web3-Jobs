Injective provides a platform for decentralized trading of various financial instruments, including derivatives. The protocol allows users to create and trade customized derivatives without the need for intermediaries. Injective aims to offer a fully decentralized trading experience while maintaining high throughput and low latency.

The platform supports a range of trading features, including limit orders, market orders, and stop-loss orders. Injective is built on the Cosmos SDK, which enables interoperability with other blockchains. The protocol has gained attention for its new approach to decentralized finance and its focus on user experience.
