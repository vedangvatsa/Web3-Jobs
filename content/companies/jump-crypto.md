Jump Crypto is the dedicated digital asset and Web3 infrastructure division of Jump Trading, a globally recognized proprietary trading firm. Leveraging decades of quantitative trading expertise, Jump Crypto operates as a major liquidity provider and active participant in decentralized ecosystems.

## Mission and Activities
In addition to traditional market making and quantitative trading, Jump Crypto is deeply involved in building and securing decentralized infrastructure. They actively contribute code, capital, and research to various foundational Web3 protocols, focusing on network scaling, security solutions, and cross-chain interoperability.
