Kraken is one of the longest-running cryptocurrency exchanges, founded in 2011 by Jesse Powell. The platform supports 200+ cryptocurrencies and offers spot, futures, and margin trading.

## Products and Services

- **Kraken Exchange**: Spot and margin trading
- **Kraken Pro**: Advanced trading interface
- **Kraken Futures**: Crypto derivatives trading
- **Kraken NFT**: NFT marketplace
- **Kraken Staking**: Proof-of-stake rewards
