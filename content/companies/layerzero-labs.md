Creator of the LayerZero omnichain interoperability protocol enabling cross-chain messaging.

## Company Overview

LayerZero Labs builds the LayerZero protocol, an omnichain interoperability solution that enables applications to communicate across blockchains. The protocol uses Ultra Light Nodes (ULNs) for secure cross-chain messaging.
