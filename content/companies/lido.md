Lido allows users to stake their assets while retaining liquidity through tokenized representations of staked assets. It primarily focuses on Ethereum 2.0 staking, enabling users to earn staking rewards without locking up their tokens. Lido's platform supports various blockchains, including Terra and Solana.

The protocol operates through a decentralized autonomous organization (DAO), allowing community governance over key decisions. Users can participate in Lido's ecosystem by becoming node operators or engaging in governance activities. The company is actively seeking talent for roles such as DAO Ops Manager to support its growing operations.
