Luno operates as a cryptocurrency exchange, allowing users to buy, sell, and store Bitcoin and Ethereum. The platform offers a user-friendly interface and is available in multiple countries. Luno provides educational resources to help users understand cryptocurrency and its benefits.

The company has a range of job listings, including roles in business development, data science, and IT support. Luno focuses on building a diverse team and fostering a culture of innovation. The exchange aims to make cryptocurrencies accessible to everyone, promoting financial inclusion through digital currencies.
