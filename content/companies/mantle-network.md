Mantle Network is a high-performance Ethereum Layer-2 modular rollup developed by the Mantle Ecosystem. By separating execution, consensus, and data availability, Mantle delivers hyperscale performance with low transaction fees and Ethereum-grade security.

The ecosystem powers a suite of decentralized applications spanning liquid staking (mETH), decentralized finance, gaming, and infrastructure. Mantle collaborates with global developers and Web3 innovators to scale Ethereum for mainstream adoption.

### About Mantle Network
Mantle Network is pioneering modular blockchain architecture to solve scalability bottlenecks in decentralized computation. With backing from a massive decentralized treasury and community governance, Mantle enables developers to deploy complex smart contracts with exceptional speed and minimal overhead.
