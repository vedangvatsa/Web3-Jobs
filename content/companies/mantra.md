## Company Overview

MANTRA is a purpose-built Layer 1 blockchain designed for tokenization of real-world assets (RWA). Built on the Cosmos SDK, it provides a regulatory-compliant infrastructure for institutions and developers to create, trade, and manage tokenized assets.
