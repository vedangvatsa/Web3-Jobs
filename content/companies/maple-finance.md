Maple Finance provides a decentralized platform that facilitates lending and borrowing for institutional investors. The platform allows users to access capital through a network of liquidity pools. Maple Finance aims to provide a more efficient way for institutions to manage their capital needs.

The company offers various roles, including Growth Engineer and Forward Deployed Engineer - Growth, indicating a focus on expanding its user base and enhancing its platform. Maple Finance's unique approach to DeFi may attract professionals interested in contributing to innovative financial solutions.
