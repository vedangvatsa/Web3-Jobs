Match-Trade Technologies is a financial technology company that provides detailed software solutions for Forex brokers, liquidity providers, and proprietary (prop) trading firms. Established in 2013, the company specializes in delivering turnkey trading environments and proprietary technology.

## Core Products
The company's flagship product is the Match-Trader Platform, an omnichannel trading platform that features integrated TradingView charts. They also offer a strong Broker Ecosystem with CRM tools, hybrid servers for managing standard and prop trading, liquidity bridges, and an open API architecture.

Match-Trade focuses heavily on the technology side, ensuring that brokers and prop firms can launch fully branded platforms quickly and reliably.
