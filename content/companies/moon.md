Moon operates at the intersection of crypto balances and mainstream e-commerce checkout.

### Products

- **Moon Visa® virtual cards** funded with Bitcoin, Lightning, USDT, USDC, or ETH, loaded in USD for online spending
- **Merchant gift cards** and prepaid cards purchased with crypto, including discounted brand gift cards
- **Business & partners**: API documentation, partner inquiries, and merchant tooling described on paywithmoon.com

### How checkout works

Users buy or load a digital card, then check out at online merchants that accept Visa cards. Moon states that cards can be used across a large Visa merchant network, with product terms and issuer details published in its legal disclosures.
