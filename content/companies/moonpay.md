MoonPay provides crypto on-ramp and off-ramp infrastructure, enabling users to buy and sell cryptocurrencies with credit cards, bank transfers, and mobile wallets. Integrated into 100+ wallets and exchanges.

## Products and Services

- **On-Ramp**: Buy crypto with fiat currency
- **Off-Ramp**: Sell crypto for fiat
- **MoonPay Swaps**: Cross-chain token swaps
- **NFT Checkout**: Fiat-to-NFT purchases
- **Enterprise Solutions**: White-label payment widgets
