Moonshot is a consumer crypto platform that simplifies on-chain trading and token discovery. Built with a sleek mobile interface and non-custodial account infrastructure, Moonshot removes the technical hurdles typically associated with Web3.

Users can onboard with standard fiat payment rails, track trending on-chain assets, and execute swaps with instant confirmation and transparent fee structures.

### About Moonshot
Moonshot bridges consumer fintech and decentralized crypto protocols, focusing on speed, delightful UX, and reliable infrastructure. The team combines deep experience across consumer applications, high-throughput trading systems, and modern Web3 protocols.
