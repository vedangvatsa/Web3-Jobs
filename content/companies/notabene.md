Notabene specializes in compliance and regulatory solutions tailored for cryptocurrency businesses. Their platform helps companies comply with regulations related to anti-money laundering (AML) and know your customer (KYC) requirements. Notabene's tools are designed to streamline the onboarding process for users while ensuring adherence to legal standards.

The company offers various job opportunities, including roles in customer success, people operations, and business development across multiple regions such as EMEA and APAC. This indicates a growing team focused on enhancing client relationships and expanding market presence. Working at Notabene provides the chance to be part of a team that is shaping the future of compliance in the cryptocurrency sector.
