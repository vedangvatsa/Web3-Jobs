O2 is a cryptocurrency exchange focused on building superior trading infrastructure. The platform aims to offer a better exchange experience compared to traditional platforms that often rely on extensive teams rather than optimized technology.

## Products and Focus
O2 emphasizes building a better, faster, and more efficient exchange. The platform positions itself as a new solution in the crypto trading space, highlighting its autonomous and technologically advanced approach to matching engines and trade execution.

### About O2
O2 is a leading organization in the Cryptocurrency Exchange space, pioneering decentralized technologies, protocol security, and next-generation blockchain infrastructure. The company builds resilient products designed for global scalability, high performance, and trust-minimized user sovereignty.

Operating across international digital asset markets, O2 collaborates with developers, institutional partners, and ecosystem contributors to advance open-source software, decentralized finance, and distributed consensus. The organization fosters a high-impact engineering and product culture focused on shipping secure, user-centric Web3 applications, continuous technological innovation, and sustainable ecosystem growth.
