OKX is one of the world's largest cryptocurrency exchanges, offering spot and derivatives trading alongside a self-custody Web3 wallet. The platform supports 300+ cryptocurrencies across multiple blockchain networks.

## Products and Services

- **OKX Exchange**: Spot, margin, and derivatives trading
- **OKX Wallet**: Multi-chain self-custody wallet with DEX aggregation
- **OKX Web3**: Decentralized marketplace and NFT platform
- **OKX Earn**: Staking and DeFi yield products
- **OKX Chain (X Layer)**: Ethereum L2 built with Polygon CDK
