OpenSea is the largest NFT marketplace by volume, supporting the buying, selling, and discovery of digital collectibles across Ethereum, Polygon, Solana, Base, and more.

## Products and Services

- **NFT Marketplace**: Buy, sell, and auction NFTs
- **OpenSea Pro**: Professional trading tools
- **OpenSea Studio**: Creator tools for launching collections
- **Multi-chain Support**: Ethereum, Polygon, Solana, Base
