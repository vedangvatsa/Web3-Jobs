OpenZeppelin is known for its open-source framework that helps developers build secure smart contracts. The company offers a suite of tools and libraries that enhance the security of blockchain applications. Their products include the OpenZeppelin Contracts library, which contains reusable and secure smart contract components.

The company also provides security audits and consulting services to projects in the blockchain space. OpenZeppelin's expertise is recognized in the industry, making it a trusted partner for many blockchain developers and organizations. They currently have job listings for Lead Institutional Sales in Europe and APAC, indicating their expansion in the institutional market.
