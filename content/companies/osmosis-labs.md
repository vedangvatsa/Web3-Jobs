Osmosis Labs builds and maintains Osmosis, the leading decentralized exchange and automated market maker (AMM) protocol for the Cosmos ecosystem. Osmosis connects disparate blockchains via the Inter-Blockchain Communication (IBC) protocol, offering seamless token swaps, liquidity provision, and cross-chain composability.
