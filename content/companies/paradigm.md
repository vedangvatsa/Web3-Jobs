Paradigm is a premier research-driven crypto and Web3 venture investment firm. Founded by Matt Huang and Fred Ehrsam, Paradigm partners with world-class entrepreneurs and engineers building the future of open finance, cryptography, and decentralized computing.

In addition to funding leading protocols and startups, Paradigm is renowned for its in-house research and engineering contributions, creating foundational open-source tools such as Foundry, Reth (Rust Ethereum execution client), and Artemis.

### About Paradigm
Paradigm takes a deeply technical, hands-on approach to venture building in crypto. Its multidisciplinary team of researchers, cryptographers, security engineers, and operators works closely with portfolio companies to advance protocol design, tokenomics, mechanisms, and software architecture.
