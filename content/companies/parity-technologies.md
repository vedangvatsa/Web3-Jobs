Parity Technologies is known for its work on the Polkadot network, which enables interoperability between different blockchains. The company focuses on building infrastructure that supports decentralized applications and services. Their products include Substrate, a framework for building custom blockchains, and the Polkadot Relay Chain, which connects various blockchains.

The company is involved in various initiatives aimed at enhancing blockchain technology and promoting decentralized governance. Parity Technologies actively contributes to open-source projects and collaborates with other organizations in the blockchain space. They offer roles that focus on new technologies, such as AI, which reflects their commitment to integrating advanced solutions into their offerings.
