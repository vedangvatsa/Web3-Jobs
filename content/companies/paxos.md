Paxos is a financial technology company that offers blockchain infrastructure for various financial services. The company specializes in enabling the issuance, trading, and settlement of digital assets. Paxos operates a regulated cryptocurrency exchange and provides a stablecoin, known as Pax Dollar (USDP), which is pegged to the US dollar.

Paxos is known for its partnerships with major financial institutions, enabling them to integrate blockchain technology into their operations. The company aims to enhance the efficiency and transparency of financial transactions through its products. Paxos is actively hiring for various positions, including software engineers and corporate development roles, indicating its growth and commitment to expanding its team.
