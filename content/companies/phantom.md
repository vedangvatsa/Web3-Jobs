Phantom is a self-custody crypto wallet supporting Solana, Ethereum, Bitcoin, Polygon, and Base. It is the most popular wallet in the Solana ecosystem with millions of active users.

## Products and Services

- **Phantom Wallet**: Browser extension and mobile wallet
- **Token Swaps**: Built-in DEX aggregator
- **Staking**: Native SOL staking
- **NFT Gallery**: View and manage NFT collections
