Pluralis Research is pioneering the frontier of decentralized artificial intelligence and open protocol learning. Through its flagship project Agora, Pluralis has demonstrated permissionless, large-scale distributed training of foundational LLM models (such as 8B parameter models) over asynchronous, geo-distributed networks of independent GPU workers.

### About Pluralis Research
Pluralis combines distributed systems engineering, cryptographic verification, and machine learning post-training techniques to ensure that future artificial intelligence models remain open, censorship-resistant, and collaboratively trained by a global network of contributors.
