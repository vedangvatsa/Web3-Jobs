Polychain Capital is one of the most prestigious and influential investment firms dedicated exclusively to the cryptocurrency and blockchain sector. Founded in 2016 by Olaf Carlson-Wee (the first employee at Coinbase), Polychain operates both as a hedge fund and a venture capital firm.

## Investment Strategy
Polychain invests in early-stage protocols, decentralized networks, and foundational Web3 infrastructure. Known for its highly technical approach and deep fundamental research, the firm actively participates in the ecosystems it funds, running validators and contributing to network governance to foster long-term growth.
