Polygon Labs builds Ethereum scaling solutions including Polygon PoS, Polygon zkEVM, and the Polygon CDK. The ecosystem is one of the most widely used L2 networks with thousands of dApps.

## Products and Services

- **Polygon PoS**: Proof-of-stake sidechain for Ethereum
- **Polygon zkEVM**: Zero-knowledge EVM-equivalent rollup
- **Polygon CDK**: Chain Development Kit for custom L2s
- **AggLayer**: Unified liquidity layer connecting L2 chains
