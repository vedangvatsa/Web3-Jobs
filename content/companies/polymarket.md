Polymarket is a decentralized prediction market where users trade on outcomes of real-world events. Built on Polygon, it became one of the most-used crypto applications during the 2024 U.S. presidential election.

## Products and Services

- **Prediction Markets**: Binary outcome markets on current events
- **Order Book Trading**: CLOB-based trading for prediction shares
- **Event Markets**: Politics, sports, crypto, culture
- **API**: Programmatic access to market data
