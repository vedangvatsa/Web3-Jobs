PwC is one of the world's leading professional services networks, providing advisory, assurance, tax, and strategy services to enterprise clients and sovereign institutions across global markets.

### Web3, Crypto & Digital Asset Practice
PwC's specialized Web3 practice assists global financial institutions, asset managers, and crypto native projects with:
- **Digital Asset Assurance & Audit:** Independent financial audits, proof-of-reserve validations, and SOC control attestations for digital asset custodians and exchanges.
- **Crypto Tax & Financial Reporting:** Enterprise tax structuring, regulatory compliance, and cross-border digital asset reporting.
- **Blockchain Technology Consulting:** Smart contract security reviews, decentralized finance (DeFi) architecture, and enterprise DLT integration.
