Ramp is a finance automation and spend management platform that helps businesses save time and money. While traditionally focused on fiat corporate cards and expense management, Ramp has actively expanded its infrastructure to support crypto-native organizations and stablecoin integrations.

## Crypto Initiatives
Ramp provides solutions for businesses to hold, manage, and transact in stablecoins like USDC alongside their traditional USD operations. The company has developed features that allow corporate finance teams to use stablecoin-backed cards, pay vendors globally with lower fees, and bridge the gap between traditional banking rails and blockchain technology.
