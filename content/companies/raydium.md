Raydium is an automated market maker (AMM) and foundational liquidity protocol built on the Solana blockchain. Providing standard AMM pools, concentrated liquidity market maker (CLMM) positions, and automated routing, Raydium serves as a primary liquidity hub for Solana DeFi tokens, meme coins, and ecosystem launches. Its high-throughput smart contracts take full advantage of Solana speed and low transaction fees. The team prioritizes AMM mathematical efficiency, Solana program development in Rust, and intuitive trading UI design.
