Decentralized GPU rendering network for AI, 3D rendering, and visual computing.

## Company Overview

The Render Network is a decentralized GPU computing platform connecting artists and developers who need rendering power with GPU providers. It supports AI inference, 3D rendering, motion graphics, and VFX workloads. Originally on Ethereum, it migrated to Solana in 2023.
