Rhino.fi (formerly DeversiFi) is a frictionless multi-chain Layer-2 DeFi gateway and cross-chain bridge. Founded by Will Harborne and Ross Jones, Rhino.fi enables users to explore, swap, bridge, and earn across dozens of blockchain networks without network switching, high gas fees, or complex bridge friction.

### About Rhino.fi
Rhino.fi leverages smart contract liquidity routing and zero-knowledge batching to execute near-instant asset transfers between Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, Polygon, Avalanche, Solana, and beyond. Designed for self-custody and high capital efficiency, Rhino.fi acts as an all-in-one hub for multi-chain decentralized finance.
