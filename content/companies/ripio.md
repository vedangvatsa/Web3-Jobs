Ripio is one of Latin America's pioneer blockchain and crypto platforms, offering digital asset trading, payments, Web3 wallet solutions, and enterprise blockchain infrastructure.

### About Ripio
Founded in Argentina in 2013 by Sebastián Serrano and Luciana Cavanagh, Ripio has expanded across Latin America to provide financial services powered by blockchain technology. The platform offers multi-currency crypto wallets, fiat on-and-off ramps, OTC trading desks, crypto debit cards (Ripio Card), and Web3 gateway infrastructure tailored for individuals and businesses across emerging markets.

Ripio is also the builder of LaChain, an Ethereum-compatible Layer-1 blockchain tailored for Latin American use cases, accelerating financial inclusion and decentralized technology adoption across the region.
