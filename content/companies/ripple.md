Ripple provides enterprise blockchain solutions for global payments. The company's network, RippleNet, enables financial institutions to process cross-border payments using the XRP Ledger.

## Products and Services

- **RippleNet**: Global payment network connecting banks and payment providers
- **On-Demand Liquidity (ODL)**: Uses XRP for real-time cross-border settlement
- **Ripple Custody**: Digital asset custody for enterprises
- **RLUSD**: Ripple's USD-pegged stablecoin
- **XRP Ledger**: Open-source, decentralized blockchain
