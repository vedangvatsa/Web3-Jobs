Securitize offers a platform that enables companies to issue and manage digital securities. Their services include compliance, investor onboarding, and secondary trading solutions. Securitize aims to simplify the process of raising capital through tokenization while ensuring adherence to regulatory requirements.

The company focuses on providing a secure and efficient way for businesses to access capital markets. They support various asset classes, including private equity and real estate. Securitize's technology allows for the creation of digital securities that can be traded on blockchain networks, enhancing liquidity for traditionally illiquid assets.
