High-performance Layer 1 blockchain optimized for trading and DeFi.

## Company Overview

Sei is a Layer 1 blockchain built from the ground up for trading. It features a built-in central limit order book (CLOB), twin-turbo consensus for 390ms finality, and parallelized EVM execution. The Sei Foundation oversees ecosystem growth.
