The platform aims to optimize transaction speeds and reduce latency, making it suitable for trading and other financial services. Sei utilizes a unique architecture that enhances the efficiency of on-chain order matching and settlement.

The company is actively expanding its team, currently listing positions such as General Counsel. This indicates a commitment to building a robust legal framework as it grows. Working at Sei offers opportunities to engage with innovative blockchain technology in the DeFi space.
