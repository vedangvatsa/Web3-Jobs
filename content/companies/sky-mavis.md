Sky Mavis is a technology company known for developing Axie Infinity, a blockchain-based game that allows players to collect, breed, and battle fantasy creatures called Axies. The game utilizes non-fungible tokens (NFTs) to represent ownership of Axies and in-game assets. Sky Mavis aims to create a new economic model for gaming, enabling players to earn cryptocurrency through gameplay.

The company has a range of job openings, including positions such as Office Administrator, Security Intern, Business Development Specialist, and Cloud Security Engineer. These roles indicate a focus on expanding their operational capabilities and enhancing security measures. Sky Mavis is actively involved in the Web3 space, contributing to the growth of decentralized gaming and the integration of blockchain technology into entertainment.
