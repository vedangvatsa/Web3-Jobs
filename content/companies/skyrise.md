Skyrise is a Web3 growth, attribution, and on-chain user engagement platform that helps decentralized applications identify, acquire, and retain real active users. Combining blockchain wallet analytics with targeted questing, rewards distribution, and conversion tracking, Skyrise enables protocols to build sustainable community growth without bot spam. The team blends growth marketing psychology, full-stack web engineering, and on-chain data indexing.
