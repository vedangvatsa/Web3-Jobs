Spiko is a pioneering European tokenized asset protocol that brings traditional sovereign treasury bills and money market funds directly on-chain. Regulated under the French financial markets authority (AMF) and European legal frameworks, Spiko issues yield-bearing digital fund units (such as EUTB and USTB) on Ethereum, Base, and Solana.

### About Spiko
Spiko bridges traditional asset management with decentralized finance, allowing institutional investors, treasuries, and protocols to hold regulated, liquid, and yield-generating government debt on-chain with continuous compound interest and seamless 24/7 secondary liquidity.
