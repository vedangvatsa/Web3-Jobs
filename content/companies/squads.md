Squads is a financial technology company building products and APIs for the stablecoin economy on open, decentralized rails. Built on open infrastructure, global money, and internet capital markets, Squads delivers modern financial services to businesses, individuals, and developers.

### Core Ecosystem Products
- **Squads Multisig**: The premier self-custodial smart account platform on Solana, securing over $10 billion in value across 450+ leading protocols and ecosystem teams including Jito, Jupiter, Drift, Kamino, and Helius.
- **Altitude**: A global business account enabling companies across 200+ countries to save, earn, and move USD, EUR, and stablecoins with corporate cards and spend management.
- **Fuse**: A mobile personal finance application designed for stablecoins and tokenized assets.
- **Grid**: The underlying stablecoin infrastructure and programmable API platform powering smart accounts, payments, and yield.
