Stork Network is an ultra-low-latency decentralized oracle network designed specifically for the performance demands of on-chain trading, derivatives, and algorithmic decentralized finance.

By utilizing high-frequency data feeds and off-chain websocket streaming with on-demand on-chain verification, Stork provides sub-second price updates without bloating blockspace or incurring excessive gas costs.

### About Stork Network
Stork Network connects institutional liquidity providers, market makers, and trading venues directly to on-chain smart contracts. Its modular oracle architecture enables builders on EVM, Solana, and Layer-2 rollups to build reactive, robust decentralized exchanges with institutional-grade data fidelity.
