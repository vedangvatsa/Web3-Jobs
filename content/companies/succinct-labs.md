Succinct Labs is building zero-knowledge proof infrastructure to make ZK development fast, accessible, and practical for every developer. Its flagship product, SP1, is a high-performance zkVM that executes normal Rust code with state-of-the-art proving speed.

Paired with the Succinct Prover Network, developers can outsource heavy ZK proof computation to a decentralized, competitive marketplace of high-performance GPU provers.

### About Succinct Labs
Succinct Labs is transforming how blockchain bridges, rollups, coprocessors, and decentralized applications achieve verifiable computation. Trusted by leading teams across Ethereum, Celestia, Polygon, and Mantle, Succinct turns cutting-edge cryptography into intuitive developer tooling.
