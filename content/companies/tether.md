Issuer of USDT, the world's largest stablecoin by market capitalization.

## Company Overview

Tether is the company behind USDT, the most widely used stablecoin in the cryptocurrency ecosystem with a market cap exceeding $140 billion. USDT is pegged 1:1 to the US dollar and is the most traded cryptocurrency by volume. Tether also issues stablecoins pegged to the euro (EURT), Chinese yuan (CNHT), and Mexican peso (MXNT). The company has expanded into Bitcoin mining, AI infrastructure, and education through Tether Edu.
