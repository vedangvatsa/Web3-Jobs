Toku is a financial technology platform providing crypto payroll, token compensation management, and global tax compliance infrastructure for Web3 protocols and organizations.

The company builds legal and financial tools enabling decentralized teams to manage token grants, tax withholding, and multi-jurisdictional employment compliance.
