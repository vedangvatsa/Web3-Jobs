Tools for Humanity (TFH) is a technology company built to ensure a more just economic system. They are the initial creators and lead contributors to the Worldcoin protocol, a privacy-preserving identity and financial network.

## Mission and Products
The company's primary focus has been developing the necessary hardware and software infrastructure for Worldcoin, including the Orb - a biometric imaging device used to verify humanness - and the World App, a wallet designed to bring digital identity and global finance to everyone.

Tools for Humanity envisions a future where humans and AI can be distinguished securely, allowing for fair global distribution of digital assets and identity verification without compromising user privacy.
