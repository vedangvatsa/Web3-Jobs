Uma is a decentralized finance protocol that enables the creation of synthetic assets. It utilizes a unique technology called Optimistic Oracle, which allows off-chain data to be used on-chain. This feature supports a wide range of financial applications, including derivatives and other complex financial instruments.

The protocol is designed to provide users with the ability to create, trade, and manage synthetic assets in a decentralized manner. Uma aims to enhance the accessibility and efficiency of financial markets by allowing anyone to create their own financial contracts. The platform is built on Ethereum, use its smart contract capabilities. 

Uma is actively seeking talent for various roles, including analytics engineering, to support its growing ecosystem. Working at Uma offers the opportunity to be part of a team that is innovating in the DeFi space.
