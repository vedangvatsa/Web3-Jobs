Unlimit is a global financial technology company bridging traditional banking, merchant acquiring, and Web3 payments. Founded in 2009 by Kirill Evstratov, Unlimit operates one of the world's broadest proprietary payment networks, serving businesses across Europe, the Americas, Africa, and APAC.

### About Unlimit
Through Unlimit Crypto, the firm provides seamless fiat on-and-off ramp APIs that allow decentralized applications, exchanges, and Web3 wallets to onboard users via credit cards, bank transfers, and local domestic payment methods across more than 150 countries.
