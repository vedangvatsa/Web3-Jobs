Walrus Foundation is a non-profit organization that supports open-source projects related to blockchain technology. The foundation aims to build collaboration and innovation within the blockchain community. They provide resources and funding to developers and projects that align with their mission of promoting open-source solutions.

The foundation currently has job listings, including a position for a Technical Content Strategist on a contract basis. This role involves creating and managing content that communicates the foundation's initiatives and projects effectively. Working at Walrus Foundation offers an opportunity to contribute to meaningful blockchain projects and engage with a community of developers and innovators.
