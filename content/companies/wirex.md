Wirex is a pioneer in digital payment solutions, bridging traditional fiat rails with cryptocurrency economies. Founded in 2014 by Pavel Matveev and Dmitry Lazarichev, Wirex launched the world's first crypto-enabled payment debit card, allowing users to spend crypto and fiat currencies seamlessly at over 80 million merchants worldwide.

### About Wirex
Wirex serves over 6 million customers across the UK, EEA, and APAC with multi-currency accounts, Mastercard and Visa debit cards, Cryptoback rewards, and high-yield savings products. Wirex also develops Wirex Pay, an institutional-grade decentralized payment chain built on Polygon CDK for non-custodial card issuance and global stablecoin settlements.
