Wynd Labs is a Web3 and AI infrastructure organization building decentralized data collection networks and distributed web crawling pipelines.

### About Wynd Labs & Grass
The engineering team powers **Grass**, an incentivized Layer 2 data network that enables node operators to share unused bandwidth. This network feeds web-scale, clean, and verifiable datasets directly to institutions training frontier artificial intelligence models.

### Technology & Architecture
Wynd Labs focuses on low-latency data extraction, distributed crawler architecture, NoSQL storage optimization, and cryptographic proof-of-bandwidth mechanisms. Their infrastructure processes billions of media files, web transcripts, and audio streams while preserving privacy and network integrity.
