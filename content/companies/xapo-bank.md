Xapo Bank offers a combination of traditional banking services and cryptocurrency custody. The company focuses on providing secure storage for digital assets, ensuring that clients can manage their cryptocurrencies safely. Xapo Bank aims to bridge the gap between traditional finance and the digital asset economy.

The company has a range of job openings, including positions for Software Engineer Intern, Product Project Manager, and Investment Manager. These roles are designed for individuals interested in contributing to the development of new banking solutions in the crypto space. Xapo Bank promotes a remote work culture, allowing employees to work from anywhere.
