Zipline operates a drone delivery service that focuses on transporting medical supplies. The company is known for its work in healthcare logistics, particularly in remote areas. Zipline's drones have been used to deliver blood, vaccines, and other essential medical products to hospitals and clinics.

The company has established operations in several countries, including Rwanda and Ghana, where it has significantly improved access to healthcare. Zipline's technology aims to reduce delivery times for critical medical supplies, which can be crucial in emergency situations. 

Zipline offers various job opportunities, including roles in engineering, logistics, and operations, reflecting its commitment to innovation and efficiency in healthcare delivery.
