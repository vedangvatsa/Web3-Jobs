**Yield curve** in DeFi refers to the relationship between lending interest rates and maturities. Short-term lending (1 day) might yield 3% APY while long-term (1 year) might yield 8% APY. The curve shape indicates market expectations: upward-sloping curves suggest rates will rise; inverted curves suggest recession. In traditional finance, yield curves are fundamental to pricing fixed-income securities. In DeFi, yield curves are emerging as protocols (Aave, Compound) offer loans at different maturities. Understanding yield curves helps predict market expectations and find inefficient pricing.

## How Yield Curves Work

The mechanics:

**Maturity Terms**: Different loan lengths have different interest rates.

**Rate Discovery**: Market forces determine rates. If many want 1-year loans but few want 1-month, 1-year rates rise relative to 1-month.

**Expectations**: Curve shape reflects market expectations about future rates.

**Duration Risk**: Longer maturities carry more risk (inflation, default, interest rate changes). They earn higher yields to compensate.

**Borrower Preferences**: Borrowers prefer short-term loans (less long-term risk). They'll pay more for shorter maturities.

Yield curves are discovered through supply/demand for loans at different maturities.

## Yield Curve Shapes

Curves indicate market expectations:

**Upward-Sloping** (Normal): Short rates low, long rates high. Indicates expectations of rising rates or risk premiums increasing over time. Most common historically.

**Flat**: Short and long rates similar. Transition period—market uncertain about direction.

**Inverted**: Long rates below short rates. Market expects future rates to fall. Often precedes recessions. U.S. Yield curve inverted 2022, preceding 2023 banking crisis.

**Humped**: Mid-term rates highest. Less common, indicates complex expectations.

Curve shapes communicate market expectations about future economics.

## DeFi Yield Curves

DeFi examples:

**Compound**: Allows variable and fixed-rate lending. Fixed-rate implies yield curve relationship between rates.

**Aave**: Offers variable rates. Fixed-rate APY varies with maturity through protocol design.

**Notional**: Protocol specifically enabling fixed-rate lending across maturities, creating explicit yield curves.

**Element Finance**: Protocol focusing on yield curve discovery and fixed-income trading.

Emerging DeFi protocols enabling borrowers to lock in rates at specific maturities.

## Yield Curve Applications

Uses in DeFi:

**Rate Forecasting**: Steep curve suggests rising rates; flat suggests stability.

**Arbitrage**: Inefficient pricing between maturities creates arbitrage opportunities.

**Duration Matching**: Borrowers and lenders can match duration preferences.

**Fixed Income Trading**: Yield curve enables fixed-income protocols similar to traditional bond markets.

**Risk Management**: Curve steepness indicates term risk premiums, useful for risk assessment.

Understanding curves enables sophisticated DeFi strategies.

## Best Practices

Understanding yield curves:

**Monitor Curve Shape**: Watch whether curve is upward-sloping, flat, or inverted.

**Compare Maturities**: Check rates across different maturities to find opportunities.

**Understand Risk**: Longer maturities carry more risk. Yield differences reflect risk premiums.

**Use Intelligently**: Match your needs to appropriate maturity rather than chasing highest yield.
