## The Data Wall

Modern AI models are extremely data hungry. Models like GPT-4 were trained on massive swaths of the open internet: Reddit, Wikipedia, GitHub, and millions of websites.

However, the industry is hitting a "data wall." AI companies have essentially exhausted the free, public internet. To make the next leap in intelligence, models need specialized, high-quality data that isn't sitting openly online—think medical records, expert-level coding logic, or real-time human feedback.

## Centralized Sourcing

Currently, AI companies solve this by hiring contractors through platforms like Scale AI. But this process is slow, centralized, and expensive. Furthermore, the humans providing the data get paid a flat wage, while the AI companies capture all the upside.

## Token-Incentivized Data Networks

Crypto fixes this through **Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks (DePIN)** and Data Markets.

Instead of a centralized company hiring contractors, a protocol can issue a token to incentivize humans globally to contribute data.
1. **Contribution:** Users connect a browser extension or app, and contribute their data (e.g., browsing history, specialized knowledge, or sensor data).
2. **Verification:** Other nodes on the network verify the quality of the data.
3. **Reward:** Users are paid in tokens.
4. **Consumption:** AI companies buy this aggregated data using the same token.

Because the contributors earn tokens, they own a piece of the network they are helping to build.

## Examples in the Wild

- **Vana:** A network that allows users to pool their private data (like their Reddit or Twitter exports) and sell it collectively to AI labs.
- **Grass:** A decentralized network that pays users for their unused internet bandwidth, which is then used to scrape AI training data from the web.

Decentralized data markets ensure that the economic value created by AI training data flows back to the people who actually produced it.
