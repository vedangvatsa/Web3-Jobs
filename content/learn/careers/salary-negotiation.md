## Understanding Web3 Compensation

Web3 compensation typically has three components:

1. **Base Salary** (cash, paid monthly)
2. **Token Allocation** (protocol tokens, vested over time)
3. **Benefits** (health insurance, remote work budget, conference budget)

The ratio between cash and tokens varies widely:

| Company Stage | Typical Cash | Typical Token | Total Comp Range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Early startup (pre-token) | $120-180K | Large token grant | $200-400K |
| Growth stage | $150-250K | Moderate tokens | $250-500K |
| Established protocol | $180-350K | Smaller tokens | $300-600K |
| Crypto-native fund | $200-400K | Carry/co-invest | $300K-1M+ |

## Token Compensation: What to Watch

### Vesting Schedule
Standard: 4-year vest with 1-year cliff.
- **Year 0-1 (cliff):** Nothing unlocks.
- **After cliff:** Tokens unlock monthly or quarterly.
- If you leave at month 11, you get zero tokens.

### Price Basis
Companies often quote token grants at the "current price" or a "30-day average." Ask specifically:
- At what price is my grant valued?
- Is the number of tokens fixed, or does it float with price?
- What happens if the token price changes significantly before my start date?

### Liquidity
Can you actually sell these tokens?
- Is the token listed on major exchanges?
- Are there lockup restrictions beyond vesting?
- Is there sufficient trading volume to sell your vested tokens without significant price impact?

### Tax Implications
Token compensation creates complex tax situations:
- In many jurisdictions, tokens are taxed as income when they vest (at the market price on vesting day), regardless of whether you sell them.
- If the token drops after vesting, you may owe taxes on value you never received.
- Consult a crypto-savvy tax advisor before accepting a token-heavy offer.

## Negotiation Strategies

### 1. Prioritize Cash in Bear Markets
When crypto is down, tokens are cheap but risky. Push for higher base salary and treat tokens as upside.

### 2. Negotiate Token Count, Not Dollar Value
If the token is volatile, negotiate a fixed number of tokens rather than a dollar-denominated grant. In a bull market, a fixed token count can appreciate significantly.

### 3. Ask for Accelerated Vesting
Negotiate for a shorter cliff (6 months instead of 12) or accelerated vesting on acquisition/token price milestones.

### 4. Request a Signing Bonus
Especially useful to cover the cliff period where you receive no tokens. A cash signing bonus reduces your risk.

### 5. Evaluate the Protocol's Revenue
Does the protocol generate real revenue? Protocols with genuine product-market fit are more likely to sustain their token value.

## Red Flags in Offers

- **"Unlimited token upside"** without disclosing current token supply, FDV, or emission schedule.
- **No vesting calendar** or vague language about when tokens actually vest.
- **Token not yet launched** with no clear timeline - you are betting on a future token that may never materialize.
- **Extremely high token-to-cash ratio** (e.g., $80K cash + $400K tokens) - this transfers all risk to you.

## Key Takeaways

- Always evaluate Web3 compensation in terms of the worst-case token scenario.
- Tokens with vesting cliffs are illiquid and risky - negotiate accordingly.
- Push for higher cash in bear markets and token count in bull markets.
- Understand the tax implications of token vesting before accepting.
- A signed offer with specific token counts, vesting dates, and price basis is essential.
