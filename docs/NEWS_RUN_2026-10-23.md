
These are orphan-image handling errors in the draft-isolation stage. They are not evidence that the associated stories passed editorial review, and they are unrelated to Google News eligibility or publisher enrollment. Some leads were also deferred after source GETs returned 403, but those are separate from the fatal validation errors.

The workflow's `continue-on-error` handling let it preserve evidence before the final **Fail incomplete publication** step failed the job. Therefore, intermediate step conclusions must not be interpreted as successful publication. The combined deploy job was skipped; the tracked deployment marker remained October 22.

The runner uploaded `news-research-39896419085-1` (artifact ID `12478308163`, ZIP SHA-256 `8d12c16dfd26df8403717fdd5c4609183399e1b94b0bae290e10c72ad2d36a98`). Recovery needs the preserved writer/draft ownership evidence, inspection of the two orphan image files, safe isolation of owned unpublished artifacts, and fresh editorial/source validation before any publication. No news recovery or extra deployment was dispatched during this diagnosis.

## Repair

- Untracked image-only outputs in the news namespace now go to the same content-addressed pending archive as unfinished drafts. Exact bytes and hashes are retained, with `approved: false`, `mustReview: true`, and an explicit orphan-image marker. Unknown ownership remains unknown. They never enter the publication file list.
- Ready article image references, tracked/baseline/published assets, ignored files, symlinks, hard links and unsafe recovery paths retain their blocking checks.
- `news-research.mjs checkpoint` records candidate/slug ownership and completed source checks before image work or drafting. It leaves the candidate unreviewed, cannot count as ready, and cannot steal another candidate's slug.
- Same-day restoration recognizes unfinished checkpoints and restores them only for fresh review. Assignments prioritize checkpointed work, and repair prompts point to retained drafts rather than starting over.
- The workflow snapshots safe unpublished outputs independently of the publication validator, before uploading the recovery artifact. Thus a later validation failure does not itself discard draft files. Writer shutdown must be confirmed first.

Regression coverage reproduces both October 23 orphan filenames, with and without a ready article. It checks exact-byte retention, rerun behavior, genuine-audit requirements, unfinished checkpoint restoration, protected-file barriers and writer shutdown. This removes the observed deterministic failure; source outages, model failures and genuine editorial failures still remain visible rather than being labeled successful publication.

## Per-article failure isolation

The actual validator now reports which selected articles own each error. After the existing bounded repair attempts, the coordinator may defer only those specific failed ready drafts when at least one other ready article remains. Original ready-review evidence, source checks, error messages, text and images are retained for repair. Unreviewed candidates are not relabeled.

Every error must be accounted for by that fresh per-article audit. Unknown errors, stale evidence, missing audits, unhealthy discovery, typecheck failures, protected-file changes and an entirely failing batch cannot use this path. Isolation is attempted once, followed by a complete new audit of the remaining batch and the publisher's own final validation. Filtering errors alone never counts as success.
