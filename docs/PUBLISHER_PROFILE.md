# Hashtag Web3 publisher profile

## Positioning

Hashtag Web3 is a global Web3 community of over 100,000 professionals and a knowledge and original news publisher. It serves developers, founders, investors, operators, marketers and product teams working across blockchain and crypto.

Use **https://hashtagweb3.com/community** as the primary organizational introduction in publisher applications and correspondence.

Treat these as the publication's stated community figures and historical examples, not independently audited traffic, guaranteed distribution or evidence of an existing news-syndication agreement.

## Editorial scope

Original crypto and Web3 news, blockchain knowledge, career guides, interview preparation, salary resources and practical tools. Native news is based on source research and publication validation; the workflow includes AI-assisted research and drafting. Required platform disclosures must remain accurate. External publisher headlines displayed in the news listing are not supplied as Hashtag Web3's own articles.

The live RSS feed currently supplies headlines, summaries, dates, images and canonical links for native news. It is not a claim of full-content feed approval on MSN, SmartNews or any other platform. Platform-specific formats, rights and agreements are handled during onboarding.
