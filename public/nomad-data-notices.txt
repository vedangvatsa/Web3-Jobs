Passport entry references
The original imported matrix is retained for audit, with its MIT attribution:
https://github.com/ilyankou/passport-index-dataset
That upstream repository is archived and is not a current verification source.

The public version-3 passport files combine reviewed government policies with
attributed Passport Index dashboard snapshots (https://www.passportindex.org/).
Government rules take precedence. Source metadata distinguishes government
checks from reference retrievals; each includes its date and source URL.
Passport Index references are not described as government-verified. Live-page
facts are separately attributed; the MIT notice below covers the archived dataset.
The snapshot covers all 199 passports and 198 foreign destinations each.
Independent government verification is partial. Unresolved rules are marked unknown and have
no asserted stay allowance. Zero days means unspecified, not unlimited stay.
Temporary exemptions include their expiry and require rechecking afterwards.
Official references are linked in each passport file's sources object.
