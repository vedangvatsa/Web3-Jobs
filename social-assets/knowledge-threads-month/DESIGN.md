## Decisions

- 1200 x 1500 JPEG masters preserve the approved portrait format while keeping the 180-image delivery compact.
- The supplied wordmark and Inter file preserve the existing brand rather than introducing another identity.
- Ivory `#f5f4ef`, ink `#171719`, white and green `#176349` come directly from the approved boards. Pale green is used only as a readable accent on ink.
- Each first image establishes its thread's subject through the first post's teaching visual, rather than consuming a post with a decorative title alone.
- A short topic name, thread ID, post count and six-position progress rule keep a sequence identifiable when images are shared individually.
- Oversized headings state the learning task. The lower field contains relationships, worked examples, comparisons, blank worksheets or actual control crops.
- Arrows encode direction, dependency or a handoff. Boxes encode scope, documents or permission boundaries. Neither is decorative.
- Comparison columns distinguish concepts. Timelines distinguish sequence. Proportional bars are used only for explicitly supplied teaching quantities.
- Worksheets intentionally leave personal values blank. Bracketed values are reader inputs, never fabricated records.
- On-image example labels identify sample wording, suggested workflows and hypothetical numerical exercises.
- Genuine product controls are captured with Playwright. Tight crops exclude changing data and personal/default form values.
- Reused community photographs illustrate working spaces and formats, with publisher credits in the image and source records. They do not advertise availability or depict participants in a fictional event.
- No gradients, shadows, ornamental icons or stock terminal frames are needed. Spatial relationships carry the lesson.
- Static imagery has no motion. The review gallery reflows to one image per row on narrow screens and provides native links to full-resolution masters.
- Long canonical article paths wrap at deliberate slash/hyphen opportunities in image footers. Platform suffixes belong to publication text.

## Production boundary

`content.json` and `SOURCES.md` belong to the editorial owner. The visual generator reads the manuscript without rewriting it. All visual files stay in this directory. Asset provenance is in `assets/source-credits.json` and `assets/capture-credits.json`. `image-manifest.json` maps each master to its source post and accessible description.
