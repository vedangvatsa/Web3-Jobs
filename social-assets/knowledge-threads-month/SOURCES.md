## Manuscript and rendering conventions

- `posts[0]` opens with a useful fact or concrete problem. `posts[5]` closes with an applicable check or decision. There are no extra hook, CTA or source posts outside the six-post count.
- `text` is publishable body copy. `title`, `audience`, `sources` and `visual.brief` are editorial metadata, not additional post text.
- `linkPath` contains one editorial destination, such as `/nomads`. Append the appropriate X or Threads suffix during rendering. Do not put tracking suffixes back into the canonical data.
- The text does not embed URLs, hashtags or numbering. The validated optional rendering is `1/6 ` + text + `\n\n` + one URL, counting that URL as 23 weighted X characters. The longest such rendering is **274**, leaving room below 280. Additional copy must be counted separately.
- There are **20 linked posts using 17 distinct destinations**. Protocol threads are useful without an own-site link. Their citations remain at thread level in the manuscript.
- Each `sources[].supports[]` record maps a source to post numbers and specific claims. A fetched date records this research session, not the source's publication date.
- Worked examples are hypothetical exercises or sample wording. No personal experience, client, measured outcome or testimonial has been invented. Bracketed resume metric fields are intentional placeholders for a reader's own records, not unfinished editorial work.
