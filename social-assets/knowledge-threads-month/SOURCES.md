# Thread sources and editorial scope

Revision: October 20, 2026. The first three claimed dates remain frozen. Future threads use concrete comparisons, actual tool outputs and worked examples.

## How to read the figures

- Salary figures are annual USD calculator-model estimates, not observed salaries or employer offers.
- City budgets and scores are directory references. Broadband figures are 2026 Q1 city-level fixed-broadband data; monthly weather is climate reference data, not a forecast.
- Country lists derived from cities name the representative city and ranking method. No city score is presented as a nationwide statistic.
- Remote-market counts are a dated October 20 aggregate of explicit applicant-location restrictions, not employer headquarters or a promise that listings remain open.
- Citizenship amounts distinguish contributions, investment capital, fees and financial-resource declarations. Visa/passport statements retain nationality, purpose and duration conditions.
- Resume and invoice examples are explicitly fictional practice examples. Screenshots show the actual application with those sample inputs.
- Upcoming-event threads check identities, local dates, location, access restrictions and price against the current event catalog before publishing. Reconciliation remains read-only.
