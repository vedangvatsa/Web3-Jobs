Malaysia offers Indian tourists 30 visa-free days; Kazakhstan offers 14 per visit.

Five destinations for ordinary Indian passports, with tourist-entry rules checked October 20, 2026.

1. Malaysia: up to 30 visa-free days for Indian tourists; exemption ends December 31, 2026.

Bring a passport valid 6 months, confirmed return ticket and hotel booking. Submit Malaysia Digital Arrival Card before arrival.

2. Thailand: Indian tourists get 30 visa-free days, 2 entries/calendar year, under September 15, 2026 rules.

Submit TDAC within 3 days before arrival; bring accommodation, onward travel and THB 20,000/person.

3. Kazakhstan: Indian ordinary passports get up to 14 visa-free days per entry, capped at 42 days in any 180-day period.

A 2-week visit fits; leaving and returning does not reset the 42-day allowance.

4. Philippines: 14 visa-free days for Indian tourists, non-extendable and non-convertible.

Bring hotel, funds and onward/return proof; passport valid 6 months beyond the stay. Transit-only trips require a transit visa.

5. Mauritius: Indian passports need no visa. Tourist stays can total up to 6 months/calendar year, subject to border approval.

Bring a return ticket, confirmed stay and funds; passport must outlast the stay.
