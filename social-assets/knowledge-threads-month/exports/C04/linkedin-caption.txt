Malaysia offers Indian tourists 30 visa-free days; Kazakhstan offers 14 per visit. Here are five destinations with the actual limits and entry documents, checked October 20, 2026. Scope: ordinary Indian passports and tourism.

1. Malaysia: up to 30 visa-free days for Indian tourists, under the exemption ending December 31, 2026. Bring a passport valid 6 months, confirmed return ticket and hotel booking; submit the Malaysia Digital Arrival Card before arrival.

2. Thailand: Indian passports get 30 visa-free tourist days under the September 15, 2026 rules, limited to 2 entries per calendar year. Submit TDAC within 3 days before arrival; bring accommodation, onward travel and THB 20,000 per person.

3. Kazakhstan: Indian ordinary passports get up to 14 visa-free days per entry, with a total cap of 42 days in any 180-day period. That makes a 2-week visit possible; leaving and returning does not reset the 42-day allowance.

4. Philippines: 14 visa-free days for Indian tourists, non-extendable and non-convertible. Bring hotel, funds and onward/return proof; passport valid 6 months beyond the stay. Transit-only trips still require a transit visa.

5. Mauritius lists Indian passports as 'No visa required.' Tourist stays can total up to 6 months per calendar year, subject to border approval. Bring return ticket, confirmed stay and funds; passport must outlast the stay.
