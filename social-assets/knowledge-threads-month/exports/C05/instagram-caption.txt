Japan's nomad visa allows 6 months; Thailand's DTV is valid for 5 years, with 180 days per entry.

Compare five remote-work routes. Eligibility depends on your work, finances, nationality and application location.

1. Thailand DTV: 5-year multiple-entry visa; 180 days per entry. Remote workers/freelancers need work evidence and THB 500,000 in funds.

London also requires permanent-residence proof and police clearance.

2. Malaysia's DE Rantau pass: 3-12 months, up to 24 with renewal, for Peninsular Malaysia and Labuan.

Annual income: USD 24,000 for tech freelancers/remote employees; USD 60,000 for non-tech talent.

3. Japan's Digital Nomad visa: 6 months, no extension. Remote workers need JPY 10 million annual income and JPY 10 million medical cover.

The principal-applicant nationality list includes the US; India is not listed.

4. Costa Rica's remote-worker Estancia lasts 1 year, renewable once.

Show USD 3,000/month from overseas work for the previous year (USD 4,000 with dependants), plus USD 50,000 medical cover for the full stay.

5. Barbados Welcome Stamp: 12 months of remote work for overseas clients or employers. Expected income: at least USD 50,000 over that year.

Fee after approval: USD 2,000 individual or USD 3,000 family.
