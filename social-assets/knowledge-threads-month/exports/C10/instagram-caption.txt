Dominica's USD 200,000 fund needs USD 210,000 for the contribution + 4 published fees. Five citizenship cost comparisons for one main applicant below. These are itemized subtotals; unlisted professional, document and transaction costs remain extra.

1. Dominica EDF: USD 200,000 + 1,000 processing + 7,500 due diligence + 500 certificate + 1,000 interview = USD 210,000. Agent, passport, bank and any enhanced-check costs remain extra.

2. Antigua and Barbuda NDF: USD 230,000 + 10,000 processing + 8,500 due diligence + 300 passport = USD 248,800 for a single applicant, before agent charges.

3. Sao Tome and Principe: USD 90,000 donation + 5,000 submission + 750 citizenship documents = USD 95,750 before agent costs. Sponsored applications add USD 5,000 benefactor due diligence.

4. Saint Lucia bonds: USD 300,000 capital + 50,000 administration + 2,000 processing + 8,000 due diligence = USD 360,000 before other costs. The bonds earn no interest and must be held 5 years; fees are not bond capital.

5. Egypt: USD 500,000 Central Bank deposit + USD 10,000 non-refundable application fee = USD 510,000 upfront. The deposit is repaid after 3 years in Egyptian pounds at the Central Bank rate on repayment, without interest.
