Five citizenship cost comparisons for one main applicant.

Dominica: USD 200,000 fund + 4 fees = USD 210,000. Subtotals exclude unlisted professional, document and transaction costs.

1. Dominica EDF: USD 200,000 + 1,000 processing + 7,500 due diligence + 500 certificate + 1,000 interview = USD 210,000.

Agent, passport, bank and any enhanced-check costs are extra.

2. Antigua and Barbuda NDF, single applicant:
USD 230,000 + 10,000 processing + 8,500 due diligence + 300 passport = USD 248,800.

Agent charges extra.

3. Sao Tome and Principe: USD 90,000 donation + 5,000 submission + 750 citizenship documents = USD 95,750 before agent costs.

Sponsored applications add USD 5,000 benefactor due diligence.

4. Saint Lucia bonds: USD 300,000 capital + 50,000 administration + 2,000 processing + 8,000 due diligence = USD 360,000 before other costs.

Bonds earn no interest and must be held 5 years; fees are separate.

5. Egypt: USD 500,000 Central Bank deposit + USD 10,000 non-refundable application fee = USD 510,000 upfront.

Deposit repaid after 3 years in Egyptian pounds at the Central Bank rate on repayment, without interest.
