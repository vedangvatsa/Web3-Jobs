Dominica can require a 3-year property hold; Saint Kitts and Nevis requires 7. Here are five Caribbean citizenship routes compared by family contribution and asset conditions. USD base amounts; fees and approval checks are additional.

1. Dominica: EDF contribution is USD 200,000 alone or USD 250,000 for the applicant + 3 qualifying dependants. Approved property starts at USD 200,000; hold 3 years from citizenship, or 5 before resale to another CBI applicant.

2. Antigua and Barbuda: USD 230,000 NDF covers a family of up to 4, plus fees. Approved property starts at USD 300,000, held for 5 years after purchase unless replaced with another officially approved property.

3. Grenada: USD 235,000 NTF for up to 4 people; siblings and parents/grandparents 55 or younger cost extra. Qualifying tourism shares start at USD 270,000 plus USD 50,000; 5 years before resale to another CBI applicant.

4. Saint Kitts and Nevis: USD 250,000 SISC covers one applicant or a family of up to 4. Additional dependants add USD 25,000 under 18 or USD 50,000 at 18+. Approved developer property starts at USD 325,000 with a 7-year hold.

5. Saint Lucia: USD 240,000 NEF for applicant + up to 3 qualifying dependants. Alternative: USD 300,000 National Action Bonds held 5 years without interest, plus USD 50,000 administration; other fees extra.
