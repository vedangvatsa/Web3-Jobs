Indian passport: Nepal is visa-free; Indonesia offers a 30-day visa on arrival; Vietnam offers an advance eVisa.

Five tourist-entry examples with documents, fees and conditions, checked October 20, 2026.

1. Nepal: Indian nationals do not need a visa. Carry a valid Indian passport or Election Commission voter card.

Nepal Tourism Board says Aadhaar is not an accepted travel document.

2. Bhutan: Indian visitors need an entry permit, not a visa. Carry an Indian passport or voter ID.

A 5-night inland trip costs INR 6,000 in adult SDF (INR 1,200/night), plus accommodation and the mandatory guide.

3. Maldives: tourist visa on arrival. Bring an MRZ passport valid at least 1 month, prepaid registered accommodation and return ticket.

Submit the free IMUGA traveller declaration within 96 hours before arrival.

4. Indonesia: Indian passports qualify for B1 visa on arrival, IDR 500,000 for up to 30 days; one extension to 60 days total.

Passport valid at least 6 months and onward/return ticket required. Online e-VOA available.

5. Vietnam: Indian tourists can apply in advance for an eVisa of up to 90 days. USD 25 single-entry or USD 50 multiple-entry; no refund if refused.

Follow the granted dates and use designated eVisa border gates.
