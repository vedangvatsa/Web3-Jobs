US passport: Japan allows 90-day visa-exempt visits; Thailand now allows 30. Five tourist destinations below, with stay limits and documents. The UK's waiver still requires an ETA. Rules checked October 20, 2026.

1. Japan: US citizens receive 90 days under the short-stay visa exemption. MOFA limits this to visits without income-earning activities. A sightseeing trip fits this route; paid work needs the appropriate immigration status.

2. Taiwan: US citizens get up to 90 visa-free days, counted from the day after arrival. Your US passport must cover the stay; carry confirmed return/onward travel and submit the Taiwan Arrival Card within 7 days before arrival.

3. Canada: US citizens need neither a visitor visa nor an eTA. Visits are usually allowed for up to 6 months; the border officer can set a different end date. Travel with a valid US passport, and follow any date on the entry record.

4. United Kingdom: US tourists can visit visa-free for up to 6 months, but normally need an ETA before travel. The current ETA fee is GBP 20. Passport must cover the whole stay; show funds and plans to leave after the visit.

5. Thailand: US tourists get 30 visa-free days; max 2 exemption entries per calendar year. Free TDAC: within 3 days before arrival. Bring accommodation, onward-travel proof and THB 20,000/person or THB 40,000/family.
