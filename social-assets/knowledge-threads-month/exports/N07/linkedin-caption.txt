US passport: Japan allows 90-day visa-exempt visits; Thailand allows 30. The UK normally requires an ETA.

Five tourist destinations with stay limits and documents, checked October 20, 2026.

1. Japan: US citizens receive 90 days under the short-stay visa exemption, for visits without income-earning activities.

Sightseeing fits this route; paid work needs the appropriate immigration status.

2. Taiwan: US citizens get up to 90 visa-free days, counted from the day after arrival.

US passport must cover the stay. Carry confirmed return/onward travel; submit Taiwan Arrival Card within 7 days before arrival.

3. Canada: US citizens need neither visitor visa nor eTA. Visits are usually allowed for up to 6 months; the officer can set a different end date.

Travel with a valid US passport and follow any date on the entry record.

4. United Kingdom: US tourists can visit visa-free for up to 6 months, normally with an ETA before travel. Fee: GBP 20.

Passport must cover the whole stay; show funds and plans to leave after the visit.

5. Thailand: US tourists get 30 visa-free days; max 2 exemption entries per calendar year.

Free TDAC: within 3 days before arrival. Bring accommodation, onward-travel proof and THB 20,000/person or THB 40,000/family.
