Five announced 2027 Web3 main conferences, in date order: Hong Kong, Cannes, Dubai, Amsterdam and New York.

Dates and venues checked with organizers on Oct 20, 2026.

1. Consensus Hong Kong | Feb 1-3, 2027 | Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre.

Themes: digital assets, AI and the agentic economy. Exhibition: Feb 2-3.

2. EthCC[10] | Apr 12-15, 2027 | Cannes, France.

Palais des Festivals hosts the Ethereum community conference. Full passes cover all 4 days; student and volunteer access options are listed.

3. TOKEN2049 Dubai | Apr 21-22, 2027 | Madinat Jumeirah, Dubai, UAE.

Two-day main crypto conference and exhibition. Ticket checkout offers card or crypto payment.

4. Dutch Blockchain Week Summit | May 19-20, 2027 | Johan Cruijff ArenA, Amsterdam, Netherlands.

Main stage, panels and breakouts. The wider city week runs May 17-23; May 19-20 is the main summit.

5. ETHConf | Jun 14-16, 2027 | Javits Center, New York, US.

ETHGlobal's Ethereum conference covers stablecoins, DeFi and scaling. General passes include stages and expo; Pro adds coworking and the networking lounge.
